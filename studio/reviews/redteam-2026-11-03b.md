# Red team pass, 2026-11-03b: the three rules of the next restart

The studio's red team (studio/roles/redteam.md), the second pass of the day, run before the restart that switches on
three rules together: the player brokerage (`brokerage.enabled`, packages/engine/src/brokerage.ts), the Reserve
(`cashReserve.enabled`, packages/engine/src/cashReserve.ts) and cash now (`token.payment.cashNow.enabled`,
packages/engine/src/receivables.ts `cashNowRate`/`cashInvoice`). All three on, the live profile
(`withLiveLevers`), four-year worlds where the brief asked for them.

Main moved under the pass: it began at 8215c61 and ended at 45189bc (ruleset/config 0.20.2-phase0.35). The commits
between them that touch the engine (cd7e9d4, the tender hold and a brokerage's first orders at formation) were in
place before every result below was re-run; nothing has changed under packages/ since 4127dd1. (cd7e9d4 briefly set
`cashNow.enabled` true in the base config; 4127dd1 put it back. Not a finding.) Nothing of mine is on main and there
is no commit. Typecheck clean; `npm test`, run once at the end: 27 files, 303 tests passed (the red team's three
included). The probes drive companies only through the public `applyAction` and read the ledger; they change no
engine code.

**Headline.** The brief's named attacks mostly come back closed. Washing money between a player's two companies
through Rule B at the band's edge, passes and transfers is a net loss (the 3% fee costs about 16 times what the band
moves). The logbook counts a load once. Cash now can't be looped or stacked into a faucet. The Reserve can't be
timed around the weekly check. Money is conserved and replays are identical on every seed.

Four things are open, one of them a blocker for the restart:

1. **E2-FORM (engine, blocks the restart).** `form_brokerage` throws on well-formed input. It leaves a half-formed
   brokerage behind, and it can stop the shared world.
2. **E3-CAPINFLATE.** The Reserve's cap is inflated with refundable lease deposits: 6.1 FD of shelter per FD of
   deposit, and the deposit comes back.
3. **E3-FACBOND.** A brokerage that also owns facilities (the shipper role) gets its shipper paper treated as
   bonded broker paper. Its 75k bond doesn't cover that paper, and the NPC factor was left 185k unpaid.
4. **E3-SPLIT.** The airdrop's profit score is clamped per company before the pair is merged. That hands pairs
   about 1k FD each out of everyone else's share.

### 1a. The holes

#### E3-FACBOND: a brokerage's shipper paper is treated as bonded, but its bond doesn't cover it

- **Mechanism.**
  - Any company can add the shipper role by owning facilities (`actions.ts` `buy_facility`), and a player brokerage
    is now a broker company that can.
  - When a carrier on the factoring contract hauls that brokerage's facility freight, `bill()` treats the paper as
    bonded broker paper (`receivables.ts:356`: `bought = b.payer === 0 || … role === "broker"`). The NPC factor
    advances it on delivery.
  - Cash now treats it the same way (`receivables.ts:421` refuses only a non-broker shipper's paper).
  - The AP cap counts the paper (`receivables.ts:122`) but doesn't stop facility freight from being posted. Only
    broker bookings and passes are gated (`bookingCapBlock`, `passTender`).
  - So the brokerage's shipper paper grows past its 75k bond. The bond pays first, then an overdraft, and on
    liquidation the rest is the factor's loss (`settleBrokerPaper`, `uncoveredFd`).
- **Reach.** Live in the beta once the switch is on: the businesses layer opens on day 28, the brokerage layer on day
  91, and the factoring contract is already live. AI brokers don't buy facilities, so this is new with the
  brokerage.
- **Who gains.** The factor's loss is FD that left the reserve. The probe did not show the owner capturing it:
  `transfer_capital` refuses to move cash out of a brokerage with paper open (`brokerage.ts:191–197`), and this
  brokerage went under itself. Rank it as a break of the factoring rule's promise (economy-factoring.md: "the
  factor's advance is always matched by a payment") rather than as a profit for the attacker.
- **Smallest fix (`receivables.ts:356` and `:421`).** Treat a broker company's paper as bonded only when it is broker
  freight (the load's `brokerId` is the payer). Treat its facility freight (the load's `shipperId` is the payer)
  like any player shipper's: not bought on delivery, not cashable. `collectUpstream` (`trips.ts:399`) knows which
  branch it is in and can pass that flag to `bill()`. The other way round is to gate a broker's facility posting by
  its AP cap. That is the economy designer's choice.

#### E3-CAPINFLATE: the Reserve's cap is inflated with a refundable deposit

- **Mechanism.**
  - The cap is `capWeeks` (13) times the average of the weeks the working-capital rule keeps (`cashReserve.ts:31`,
    `weeklySpend` from `step.ts:145`). `weeklySpend` counts outflows to sinks, except the refs in `NOT_SPENDING`
    (`ledger.ts:27`).
  - An NPC lease deposit posts to a sink (`capacity.ts:40`, `trips.ts:453`: `sink:lease_deposit`, ref `deposit:`).
    So it counts as spending, though it is refunded when the truck goes back (`world.ts` `refundLeaseDeposit`).
  - `lease_truck` isn't locked in the season-close window (only purchases are).
  - So a hoarder leases extra trucks in the four weeks before a close, keeps its cash in the Reserve, and hands the
    trucks back after the close for the deposit. Its liquid shelter from the burn rises by about 6 FD per FD of
    deposit, at the cost of the trucks' rent.
- **Measured.** 182,000 FD of deposits bought 1.10M FD more shelter at the peak: 845k of Reserve cap and 260k of
  working capital. With `maxPerCompany` 10 leases (455k of deposits) it scales to about 2.7M. For a company holding
  that much idle at a post-launch close, that is about 1.1M FD a close, roughly 3,000 FD a day over the year.
- **Context.**
  - The working-capital half of this exists without the Reserve; the Reserve multiplies it about four times.
  - A stake already escapes the season close behind its 28-day exit queue, so what the hole gives a hoarder is
    *liquid* shelter past the rule's own "13 weeks of own spending".
- **Smallest fix (`ledger.ts:27`).** Add `"deposit:"` to `NOT_SPENDING`. The Equipment Finance deposit shares that
  prefix but posts to `co:pool:equipment_finance`, a transfer, so it is unaffected. The other way round is to hold the
  lease deposit in an escrow account instead of a sink.

#### E3-SPLIT: the pair's profit is clamped twice, so a brokerage's loss is dropped

- **Mechanism.**
  - At the launch each company's beta operating profit is clamped at zero first (`token.ts:558`;
    `seasonAirdrop` does the same at `token.ts:646`).
  - Only then does `onePersonOneShare` (`token.ts:836–843`) add the brokerage's to its carrier's.
  - So the person scores `max(0, carrier) + max(0, brokerage)`, not `max(0, carrier + brokerage)`. Beta brokering
    loses money (economy-brokerage.md §3), and Rule B at the band's ceiling moves more profit from the brokerage to
    the carrier at will.
- **Measured.** About 94–101k FD a seed handed to the pairs out of the rest of the pot, about 1.1k a pair, the
  largest 2.3k. Bounded by the band (±15%) and the 1% per-person cap.
- **Smallest fix (`token.ts:836`).** Merge the two companies' raw results and clamp once. That means passing
  unclamped profits from `token.ts:558` and `:646` into `onePersonOneShare`.

## 2. Break the engine

`apps/sim/src/redteam/fuzz.ts` gains `--three`, which:
- turns the three switches on;
- adds the new actions to the menu, built from real ids: forming, transfers both ways, passes and the tenders behind
  them, own tenders inside and outside the band, cash now on own and others' paper, the Reserve in and out, and a
  brokerage's broker order;
- has a carrier attacker form its brokerage the first hour it can, as a logged step;
- makes the free attacker a human player, so the player-only rules bind it.

It also checks every new action's answer against the state it was given: an ok the rule forbids, or a refusal whose
reason the state contradicts. It runs the stricter invariants `checkThree` (new in `harness.ts`: the pair link
two-way, a stake backed by its account, a launch hold never paying more than it kept, the Reserve's settling entries
agreeing with its account, no negative receivable), and it compares the replay account by account (`ledgerHash`,
new).

### HOLE E2-FORM: `form_brokerage` throws and leaves a half-formed brokerage (blocks the restart)

- **Seed.** `fuzz.ts --seeds 2 --days 220 --actions 50 --three`, day 93. Carrier co:91 had `weeksInDebt 1`,
  `tierGraceDay null` and 220,329 FD of cash. The action threw `brokerage capital: the company has paper overdue`.
  It left broker co:103 formed and linked to co:91, with cash −300 (its licence) and staked 0.
- **Mechanism.**
  - `formBrokerage` checks `canFormBrokerage` (`brokerage.ts:132–144`), the capital and the cash.
  - Then it creates the broker company and links the pair (`brokerage.ts:164–166`).
  - Only after that does it call `transferCapital` (`brokerage.ts:171`), which has guards `canFormBrokerage`
    doesn't: a carrier under its stake (`brokerage.ts:196`, any carrier with `tierGraceDay` set) and paper overdue
    (`:198`).
  - The refusal becomes `throw new Error(…) // checked above` (`brokerage.ts:172`), but it wasn't checked above.
- **What it does in the live world.**
  - **A player** who presses "form a brokerage" while in a debt week or in its stake grace: the page catches the
    throw (`apps/web/src/live.ts:504`) and shows "that order couldn't be carried out". Every page has replayed the
    half-formed brokerage by then. The owner is left with a bondless brokerage at −300, can never form a proper one
    ("one carrier and one brokerage per owner"), and the brokerage log counts capital that never moved.
  - **A bot.** The brokerage bots run in the live world (live.ts doesn't override `brokerage.bots.share` 0.25).
    `runBrokerages` checks only `canFormBrokerage` (`packages/agents/src/ruleBots.ts:1916`) and calls the action
    with no catch, inside the daily hook (`ruleBots.ts:1923`, `:1627`). A chosen bot carrier that can fund the bond
    while in a debt week or its stake grace throws out of `stepHour`. `live.ts:263–269` then marks the world broken
    and stops it on every page.
  - The economy's four-year batches never hit the bot case, so it is reachable but not observed.
- **Smallest fix (`brokerage.ts:132–144`).** Have `canFormBrokerage` refuse a carrier with `weeksInDebt > 0` or
  `tierGraceDay !== null`, the reasons `transferCapital` would give. Or check `transferCapital`'s preconditions before
  `formCompany` at `brokerage.ts:164`, so a refusal comes back clean and nothing is created.

Everything else held: money conserved (the double-entry ledger never threw), the cap identity to the cent, no negative
escrow including `co:cashreserve` and `co:cashreserve-out`, no stake above its account, no launch hold paying out
more than it kept, the Reserve's settling ledger in agreement, no refusal whose stated reason the state contradicted,
and every replay identical account by account.

**1. R16, the pay floor and the notice.**
- The floor holds on both ways to set pay: `set_staff_pay` (actions.ts:279) and offers (labor.ts:176).
- **The underpay memory can be skipped by firing.**
  - The memory is written only when an unhappy driver leaves through the notice itself (`dailyLabor`, labor.ts:328).
  - `release_staff` (actions.ts:259) charges severance only within 28 days of the hire, then `releaseStaff` clears
    `unhappyNotice` without writing the memory (labor.ts:246).
  - So an employer that fires a driver on notice dodges the 56-day +10%.
- A raise to the ask on the notice's last day withdraws the notice (labor.ts:260), and nothing stops a cut back to the
  floor the next day.
- Pay set at the floor can also drift under it as the market or the driver's skill moves. The floor binds only when pay
  is set.
- Smallest fix: write the memory when a driver on an unhappy notice is fired (labor.ts:246 or the `release_staff`
  case), and hold a withdrawn notice's raise as the `matchedPay` rule holds a rival's match (labor.ts:270–276).

**2. R17, a ceiling is a ceiling.**
- Every spot path is capped at the ceiling:
  - the dispatcher's bid at `hardCap` = ceiling × `overCeilingMax` (standingOrders.ts:91);
  - manual bids re-capped at the round (board.ts:168);
  - `place_bid` refused over it (actions.ts:853);
  - book-now never above it (freight.ts:297–325; a re-post resets both, fraud.ts:84–85).
- Contract tenders pay the contract rate by design (economy-player34.md §1a).
- **The own-carrier tender is the one path not tied to the load's ceiling.** Its band is the lane's trailing rate ×
  [0.85, 1.15] (`ownBand`, brokerage.ts:256), which can sit above the load's ceiling. It is money between one owner's
  two companies, but it is an exception to "no load clears above its ceiling". Not measured.

**3. R18, the audit.**
- What an audit reads stays on the company, not the truck (safety.ts:112, :638). So selling or scrapping a truck
  removes nothing an auditor can find; a sale clears only the truck's own on-board days (`freshPaperwork`, world.ts).
- Changing policy before an audit stops new entries but erases none.
- A bankruptcy restart keeps the safety record. Only a refile resets it (solvency.ts:184), and a refile needs a
  revocation and its bar (safety.ts:577).
- A roadside out-of-service order is spent inside the trip already under way (trips.ts:185), so returning a leased
  truck can't shed it.
- An audit's window drops what the previous audit missed (safety.ts:687–688, the director's ruling of 2026-11-03).

**4. R12, the clocks.**
- **The day's and the week's clocks are kept on the truck** (`shiftDrivenMin`, `cycleMin`: trips.ts:102–118,
  :266–269), not on the driver. `seatDriver` (world.ts:511) leaves them as they are.
- `assign_driver` (actions.ts:285) checks only that each truck is at *a* hub (`atHub`, actions.ts:227), not the same
  one.
- So a driver whose truck is out of hours, moved to a rested truck, starts a fresh day and a fresh week with no false
  log.
- A team truck's doubled clock (trips.ts:118) is consistent: removing the co-driver turns any excess into a false-log
  day (trips.ts:269).
- The restart's parking fix makes a no-mile duty day "the same stop" (sites.ts:213): one space, one night's idling, one
  patrol roll for a 34-hour restart, on the shoulder too.
- Smallest fix: keep the two clocks on the driver (Staff) and read them through the truck's driver, or refuse a
  driver's move to a truck at another hub.

**5. R8 and the dispatcher default.**
- The hire guard runs only in auto-staff and only for a human company (labor.ts:459, `hireCoverHumansOnly`).
- A human's auto-staff starts off (world.ts:645).
- `guard.dispatcherDefaultOn` is read nowhere in the engine or the agents, only by the web's `joinCompany`
