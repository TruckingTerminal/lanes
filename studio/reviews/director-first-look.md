# Director's first look (2026-11-01)

Test build 0.19.1, played as a stranger: an owner-operator out of Dallas, ten game days.

## At its best

A freight market you can read. The board shows the lane, the empty miles, my break-even, the poster's ceiling and what the lane usually clears at, over a map of real roads where my rig crawls past Houston and sleeps somewhere with a name. The ticker tells a true story: my hand-booked load lost 124 FD, the dispatcher's next one made 1,616, a night on the shoulder cost a 250 FD ticket. That is a game. Iuliia is a voice, not a tooltip.

## What fights it

- **The game plays itself.** The default dispatcher bids while the player watches; it out-earned me ten to one.
- **Nothing leaving my hub was for me.** Every Dallas load was strict vetting ("authority under 90 days") or local-first; the one open load lost money. Iuliia's first instruction, pick a load leaving your hub, can't be followed.
- **The token arrives in minute two.** The Money tab sells a $1,000 auction bid, the Dealer sells rigs for dollars, the Logbook explains an airdrop. In a test world. It reads as a crypto pitch wearing a trucking game.
- **Three words for one act.** Iuliia says bid; the board says book or counter; the ticker says "your bid". Iuliia says the dispatcher is "you, holding nothing"; the status bar says it is watching the board.
- **The result is hidden.** Net-30 means the cash line falls for a month after a good week. The Rates tab reads 2.50 and Hot at every market.
- **Thirteen tabs, and the panel moves under the hand.** The row I had open closed at 18:00.

## Cut first

1. Every dollar and token surface in the beta client: the auction, dollar prices, the Logbook, the exchange copy. Back with the server and counsel.
2. Tabs, thirteen to five: Fleet, Loads, Money, Company, Guide. The rest fold in or wait for the ladder.
3. The default auto-dispatcher for a one-truck company. The player runs the first week by hand; the dispatcher is a hire, the first thing worth paying for.

## The one question for Jeff

Would people play LANES with no token at all? If yes, the beta ships without a word about FD's price. If no, nothing we cut will matter.
