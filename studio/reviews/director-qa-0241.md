# Three calls from QA's 0.24.1 replay (director, 2026-11-12)

## 1. The night in town: rule, with words now
**The rule should reach mid-leg.** Out there, a driver plans the night before setting out: if the hours will run out
mid-leg, they stop at the last town or truck stop before that, and lose the hours in between. A setting called "stop
for the night in town" that ends on the shoulder is false. Engineering plans it in planNight: stop at the last town
or stop within the day's hours, and the economy reads the cost in its pair. It moves the bots that use the setting,
so it lands in a later ruleset.

**Words until then:** "Stops for the night in a town when a leg would begin past your hours. A long leg can still end
on the shoulder." It says when it applies; it doesn't promise every night.

## 2. The hand bid in a local hold: right as designed. Rule stays; one word owed.
The filed bid enters the opening round on the same terms as everyone. That's what I ruled on 11-09 ("yours is in that
round"), and pillar 2 wants the same rules for all. No favour.

**But a loss to a higher bid needs its reason said.** On the lost card, name what beat it: a vetting tier, the
safety record, an earlier ETA. For example: "Lost to 4.40/mi: the shipper chose a carrier with a longer safety record."
If the clearing has no such reason and awarded the higher bid anyway, that's a bug for engineering, not a design.
