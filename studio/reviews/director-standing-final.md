# Standing, final: the economy's sizes, reconciled (director, 2026-11-13)

Engineering builds from this note. Everything from director-standing-10-2026-11-13.md stands unless changed here.

## Sizes: accepted as the economy set them (rule)
- A 180-day window.
- Floors in loads per 180 days, tiers 1 to 10: 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 7 / 10 / 14 / 20 / 30.
- On time at 70% from tier 3, rising to 90% at tier 10.
- A claim, a no-show or a release costs two loads; a late delivery costs one.
- Decay after eight weeks without a load.
- Weekly review, one step at most, as before.

## One scorecard per origin market: yes. A shipper's name on it: no, with one exception (rule and words)
A scorecard named for Kessler Foods that rises when the player hauls for another shipper is false, and a dispatcher
would see it on the first load. One scorecard per market is true in another form: shippers in a market talk, and
carrier-rating services pool what they report. So:
- **The name is the market's:** "Standing with Dallas shippers". Every direct and contract load from that origin
  counts toward it.
- **The exception:** a shipper's name may front a scorecard only if every load it counts is that shipper's own
  posting. If the economy later splits the ten busiest hubs into two named shippers with halved floors, that's the
  test. Not in v1.
- Brokered loads still don't count; standing with brokers stays on the backlog.

## The perks: accepted (rule)
| Tier | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| 3 | Dock priority at the market's docks |
| 5 | Net-15 (not fee-free quick pay) |
| 7 | First call on the market's direct tenders with a day or more of life left after the round, ranked above the locals-first hold, limited to loads the truck can make |
| 9 | Contract offers and renewals |
| 10 | A dedicated-lane offer |

First call above the locals-first hold is true: a carrier in a shipper's routing guide gets the tender before the
open market, local or not.
