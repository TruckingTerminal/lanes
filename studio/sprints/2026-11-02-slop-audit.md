# Sprint 0: the slop audit (2026-11-02)

Jeff, 2026-11-01: "The game feels like pure slop in its current form." Before anything else is built, the studio
looks at what exists the way a stranger would, and the director writes down what the game is.

## Deliverables
- `playtests/2026-11-02-slop-audit.md` (QA): two sessions of the public test page, one on a phone as a dispatcher
  in Tbilisi who has never seen the game, one on a laptop as a Texas owner-operator; the first ten minutes
  minute by minute; every moment of confusion, every dead end, every bug; what felt good; a ranked list.
- `specs/first-ten-minutes.md` (UX): the screen inventory as it stands (every tab, what it's for, who needs it
  when), what a phone can carry, a proposal for the first ten minutes: the screens in order, what's on each, what
  waits, how the map and the panel relate; wireframes in words.
- `BIBLE.md` draft 1 (director): from `README.md`, `TOKENOMICS.md`, `TODO.md` and the decisions log; the cut
  list; the questions only Jeff can answer, phrased for a yes or a no.
- `reviews/art-style-note.md` (art): what in the client's chrome fights the pixel art, and the cheapest fixes.

## Done means
The producer has all four, has reconciled them into one page for Jeff, and Jeff has answered the questions.
