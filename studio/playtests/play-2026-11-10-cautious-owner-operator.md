# Play diary: the cautious owner-operator who does exactly what Iuliia says

- **Who I was:** a careful owner-operator on a phone who reads everything and does what Iuliia and What now? say,
  nothing more: the lease, the hold, the first truck, the wait, the hand-over, the first net-30 invoices. The question:
  does following every instruction ever lose money, contradict itself or dead-end?
- **Build:** lanes-play `87864b2` (Deploy c8c67db), version.json `{"build":"c8c67db"}`, ruleset 0.24.0-phase0.41.
- **Screen and speed:** phone, 375 × 812. "Day in 6 s" between What now? check-ins (about every 5 game days), "Day in
  2 s" during the wait for the truck.
- **How long:** about 45 real minutes, Thu Jan 11 to ~Fri Jul 21, Year 1 (game day ~191). I owned the truck from
  ~Jun 20, so about a month of ownership.
- **Where I ended:** Careful Coast Carriers, Los Angeles. One owned used Workhorse (40% condition when ordered), on a
  purchase loan. 6,944 FD in cash, five invoices owed on net-30, and a forecast: "your cash goes below zero around Sat
  Aug 20 … as low as −1,351 FD". June kept 8,648 FD.

## The diary

**Day 1.** The rings: Los Angeles first ("Freight is good here in January"), so LA. A new line under the hubs: "Across
the country, rates over the last week are up 24% on the four weeks before." What now?: "Book San Diego Country Estates to
Oklahoma City. 3,459 over break-even, plus your 1,142 draw as the driver: 2,576 to cash now, 2,026 toward your truck."
The load page says the same, draw named ("Cash now 2,576 FD · includes your 1,142 FD draw"). Last round's mismatch is
gone. Booked.

**Day 6, first pay.** "Yours 2,082 FD". What now? had said "2,576 to cash now", so the forecast ran 494 FD (19%) high.

**Day 6–20, following to the letter.** "Book Ashland to Dayton" (the pickup line now says "Ashland, KS · Wed Jan 17 07:00,
when the dock opens", a fix). Then "Bid on Detroit to Wheaton … 190 over break-even" (my script booked at 4.19
instead of bidding; my slip). Then "Bid on Chicago to Biddeford. 62 over break-even … 68 to cash now". I bid what the
button offered: "Lost to 2.55/mi · your bid 3.06". The default bid on a "bid" recommendation was 0.51 over the
winning price.

**Day 20, the first forced deadhead.** With the dispatcher off and no command from me, the truck began "Running empty to
Los Angeles · 1775 mi". The 21-day home rule applies even with the dispatcher off. What now? at that moment: "Step 5 of
13 · Pick the next load from Find loads, nearest first." It said nothing about the 1,775 empty miles. Cash went from
8,055 to 4,896.

**Day 30–35.** "Bid on El Cajon to Dallas instead. You lost the last one. This load clears near 5.01/mi" → "Won El Cajon →
Dallas at 5.01/mi · 6,959 FD". Then: "Truck 1 is stranded at Dallas: nothing it may take from here pays. Cheapest way
out: Oklahoma City · 203 mi empty · about 263 FD · 4 h · 1 load pays there. Next cheapest: Houston …" and a SEND TO
OKLAHOMA CITY button. That's the stranded-state fix, and it's exactly right.

**Day 40, the first truck priced.** Iuliia's page: "To your first truck: 519 FD. The truck I've priced for your first is
worn, at 40% condition … I'd wait for a sounder one. Waiting to May 23 keeps you paying the lessor 15% of gross for 85
days: about 6,680 FD … I'd keep about 8,900 FD back." So: wait for a sounder one, waiting costs 6,680, keep 8,900 back,
and you're 519 away, all at once. A careful person can't follow all four.

**Day 45–50, the second forced deadhead.** "Running empty to Los Angeles · 1687 mi" again; cash 6,550 → 2,974. What now?
meanwhile: "Step 7 of 13 · Check the condition bar; plan the rebuild before it's urgent", about a leased truck whose
lessor does the maintenance.

**Day 70, the pick is a reefer.** Iuliia and the Dealer: "You can buy once truck 906 is idle … the cheapest truck your hold
already covers: a used Workhorse with a reefer, 509k miles, 28% condition, in Goshen (Chicago market); your company
hauls dry van. … I'd wait for a sounder one." She recommends a reefer at 28% to a dry-van company, and in the same
card says wait. My truck is "truck 906" here, "Truck 1" everywhere else.

**Day 80, the third forced deadhead.** Cash 6,319 → 3,836. Then "Step 9 of 13 · Buy a rig from the used lot, with a purchase
loan if the cash is short. GO TO DEALER". The cheapest lot rig: "Cash needed now 3,823 FD"; I had 3,320. The step
can't be done that day, and the same Dealer page says to keep 9,900 back.

**Day ~85, the order.** After the next pay (5,309 FD), I ordered the lot rig on a loan. The listing said "Cash needed now
3,823 FD · ready Jun 15"; the confirm said "It costs · 1,730 FD … ready Jun 17"
([01](play-2026-11-10-cautious-owner-operator/01-order-confirm-1730-vs-listing.jpg)). Two screens, two prices, two
dates. "Left after · 3,579 FD, 1.5 weeks of cushion. Under 4 weeks of cushion after this." That's honest, and against
Iuliia's 9,900. Then, reading the log ("The dispatcher is off, so nobody bids for this truck while you're away"), I
turned the dispatcher on. Iuliia never told me to.

**Day ~85–160, the wait.** With the dispatcher on, cash went 3,579 → 17,925 → 25,592. Following What now? by hand for
80 days, cash had stayed between 3,000 and 8,000 and the truck had been dragged home empty three times. Loan payments
ran from April for a truck that arrived in June (the borrower saw the same).

**Day ~160 (Jun 20), the hand-over.** "New truck delivered: truck 1 is ready". It was handed over while the truck was loaded
LA → Denver, with no gap. 

**Day 160–191, the first month owning.** Invoices on net-30 appeared under Money with CASH IT NOW on each. Cash went
25,592 → 8,347 → 3,765 → 6,944. The forecast line is the best thing on the screen: "At this rate your cash goes below
zero around Wed Jul 19 … before your next invoice lands on Fri Jul 21. An owned truck bills on net 30". What now? said
"Step 10 of 13 · Hire a driver at a pay the market will take. GO TO STAFF". I have one truck and I drive it. GO TO
STAFF opened a locked teaser: "Staff · Opens at your second truck, or a truck without a driver". Iuliia added: "A second
truck needs a second driver. Pay decides who takes the job"
([02](play-2026-11-10-cautious-owner-operator/02-second-truck-needs-driver-with-one-truck.jpg)). I don't have a second
truck. June's report: "Maintenance and repairs 8,166 FD" (the 40% truck she both priced and warned me off), and "11
loads delivered · 82% on time · 8,497 of 421,169 miles loaded (2%)". The truck's odometer is counted as June's miles,
the same bug the borrower hit.

## The five things that most got in the way
1. The 21-day home rule pulling the truck home empty, unannounced, while What now? talks about something else.
2. Step 10 sending a one-truck owner to a locked Staff page.
3. First-truck advice that contradicts itself every time it speaks.
4. Two prices for the same order.
5. Bids that lose by half a dollar.

## The three best moments
1. "Truck 1 is stranded at Dallas … Cheapest way out: Oklahoma City · 203 mi empty · about 263 FD … SEND TO OKLAHOMA
   CITY."
2. The cash forecast after the hand-over: a date, a depth, and when the next invoice lands.
3. The hand-over itself: no idle gap, no surprise.

## What I did not understand
- Whether I should wait for a sounder truck or buy the worn one; Iuliia says both.
- Why my truck goes home on its own with the dispatcher off.
- Why the order cost 1,730 when the listing said 3,823.

## Ugly or broken
Nothing visual beyond the odometer-in-loaded-miles line.

## Would I come back tomorrow?
Yes, I own the truck now. But a person who does exactly what Iuliia says spends three months at 3–6k FD while the
dispatcher, which she never mentions, would have bought the truck in one. The guide's advice is honest about each
number and wrong about the path.

## What the game is about, as I understood it
Lease, save through the hold, buy a worn truck on credit, then survive net-30. The pieces are all there and the money
screens tell the truth. The guide walks a beginner past the one tool (the dispatcher and its home rule) that decides
whether the first months make money.
