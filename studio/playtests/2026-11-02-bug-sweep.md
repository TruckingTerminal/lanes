# Bug sweep, public build, 2026-11-02

Desktop 1280×800, built-in browser. New test world, Test Freight, small fleet, Dallas. I let it run at the faster speeds to about game day 32 (Feb 1), then went through the tabs in order. Severity: **H** a new player is hurt or misled, **M** confusing or wrong, **L** polish. Controls I pressed through script clicks, not a mouse; I did not press Bid in the opening auction, and I did not press New test world.

## Top ten (worst for a new player first)

1. **Cash goes negative in month one with no mistake.** −42.4k FD on Feb 1, "2 more weeks in debt and the company is wound up". Money shows 195.9k revenue against 67.1k costs. Net-30 is the default, so wages go out and invoices sit open (195.9k owed). Nothing on the Fleet or Money tab says so before it bites.
2. **The walkthrough and the Guide describe a different game.** Step 1 and the Guide say one leased Workhorse and 5,000 FD. The small fleet is two owned trucks and 25,000 FD. Step 9 shows "Your first truck ✓" unearned.
3. **The walkthrough advances itself.** I did nothing and it reached step 5, then 8. "7 of 13 done" shows on step 6, "10 of 13 done" on step 8. A player cannot tell what they were supposed to do.
4. **The fleet runs without the player.** Truck 2 got a five-star driver and I got a dispatcher with no confirm, while in debt (Auto-staff). The "Needs attention: no driver" card vanished on its own.
5. **Idle diagnosis is wrong.** Truck 1 "sits out rounds because other carriers bid lower". It was the driver (me) on 5 days home time at "Zwolle", a place never explained. The Load board filters "within 300 mi of Zwolle" while the truck sits in Dallas.
6. **Borrow, Lease and Factor are dead ends.** Borrow offers "up to 32.6k FD" and says "The pool doesn't have the cash". Lease says "No money down" and says "Not enough FD for the lessor's deposit". Factor says "The contract stake needs more FD".
7. **Report can't be sent.** Toast: "Test world: reports aren't sent from here." The panel promises it is "Sent with the day, your company…".
8. **Tab labels are clipped at 1280.** Each tab button is 26–31 px wide with 39–53 px of text, so a player sees "OAD", "TAFF", "NANC".
9. **Dealer contradicts itself.** "0 on the lot this week" above three listed used rigs. Trailers: "Room for 0 more" while the Fleet yard says 2 of 5 used.
10. **Empty Counter places a bid.** The field was untouched; the button placed a bid at 4.69/mi with no confirm. The empty-load board when no truck is idle says only "Loads show here when a truck is free."

## Console

`[info] map 1584×856 painted in 891 ms` was the only entry in the whole session. I also hooked `error`, `warn`, `unhandledrejection` and `window.onerror` from game day 7: zero captured after every tab and every press. No console errors.

## Confirmed from the persona playtest

Numbers are the slop audit's bug list.

- **#10 re-post, checked.** The "Re-post the load (won at 4.64)" button is live on a loaded Fleet card (I looked on day 32; it was also in the standing orders on day 7). Pressed, it **does not act**: toast "Re-brokering open on day 91 of the beta". So it only shows, and refuses, but it is a live-looking button for a feature locked for 90 days.
- **Receivables, checked.** Money shows no receivables or due days. Finance shows one total, "Owed to you 195.9k FD on open invoices", with no list and no dates. The walkthrough (step 6) points at both. Matches #9 and #14.
- **#2, #3 walkthrough ticks itself and the done-count runs ahead** (6 of 13 with "7 of 13 done"; 8 with "10"). Confirmed.
- **#5 Lease "No money down"** → "Not enough FD for the lessor's deposit". Confirmed.
- **#6 and #7 Dealer** disabled buttons with no reason; "0 on the lot" over listed rigs. Confirmed (my version: 0 vs three rigs, not 30).
- **#8 step 1 wrong for small fleet.** Confirmed.
- **#14 cash vs revenue.** Confirmed, −42.4k on day 32 against 195.9k revenue. Likely cause: net-30 invoices.
- **#4 ticker "no driver"** and **#12 truck mismatch:** truck 2's red box cleared on its own for me; not reproduced as stated.

## From the UX designer's playtest

- **Hub tap never opens the city pane: confirmed at 1366×768.** Three taps on the Dallas label: the map zoomed or did nothing, no pane. (Not tried at 375.)
- **Loads panel scrolls sideways on re-render at 1366: not reproduced.** Checked with an empty board only (no idle truck), no horizontal scroller found. Needs a board with rows to settle it.
- **Added while there:** at 1366 the walkthrough's step 9 says to buy a used rig "with a purchase loan from the Money tab". In my world the loan pool was empty, so Borrow failed ("The pool doesn't have the cash").
