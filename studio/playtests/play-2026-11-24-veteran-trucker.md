# Play test 2026-11-24: the veteran trucker

Persona: twenty-odd years over the road, some of them with my own authority. Phone in the sleeper (375 x 812).
Build 2ce9b53, server lanes-player-5. Company: Red River Haulers, home Dallas, owner-operator start.
Reached Thu Mar 1, game day 50. About 125 tool calls, 17 screenshots.

## 1. The verdict

- Would I come back tomorrow? Maybe once more. The paperwork is right (hours, settlements, claims, deadhead), so it
  talks my language. But the rates are make-believe: 10 and 13 a mile on the same board as 1.84, and that kills it for a trucker.
- I'd come back for the settlement sheet and the bidding against named carriers. I'd quit over the rates and the
  dispatcher hauling me to Delaware, then Edinburg, then Idaho, with no reason I could see.
- What it's about, as I understood it: you start leased-on to a lessor with one truck, haul spot freight you bid on
  against other carriers, save toward your own truck, then grow a fleet run by a dispatcher you set rules for.

## 3. The diary

**Thu Jan 11, 06:00 (day 1).** Title screen: a Pete-looking tractor and a diner at sunset. Nice. The first tap on
NEW TEST WORLD looked dead. I pressed Shift and it had gone through (a test-setup thing, not the game). Julia says
"Find a load, haul it, get paid, keep the truck alive." That's the job.

**Day 1, hub pick.** Three starters, then ALL HUBS, 60 cities. Miami "Freight is thin here... locals keep what there
is". Right, Florida's a dead end for reloads. I picked Dallas: "Freight leaves in every direction, and so will you."
Its card says "Ships more than it receives" and right under that "Reload: hard". If Dallas ships more than it gets,
reload is easy, so which is it? "13 loads posted last round · 4.46/mi average": spot van out of Dallas, 4.46? Brother, no.

**Day 1, the name box.** It came filled in with "Trinity River Freight". I selected all of it and typed "Red River
Haulers", and it took. If I'd tapped and typed, I'd have got "Trinity River FreightRed River Haulers". Picked the man
portrait and Owner-operator. "One leased Workhorse with a dry van... The lessor... keeps 15% of every load." Fine. Real
lease-ons take 25 to 30, but they cover the plates and the insurance too.

**Day 1, the board.** Fourteen loads. The card lays out what I'd ask a broker: loaded miles, empty miles, rate loaded
and rate all-in, pick up by, deliver by. Abilene → Kingsport 5.41/mi, 206 empty (Dallas to Abilene is about 180, OK).
Opened it: "Deliver by Sun Jan 14 16:28" and further down "Receiver opens Mon Jan 15 07:00 · about 49 h waiting at
delivery... Waiting until a dock opens is your own time." So I'm due on Sunday at a dock that opens Monday, and the 49
hours are on me. That part's real: detention is free for the shipper. A deadline before the doors open isn't, though.
Took Vegas instead: Abilene → Las Vegas, 1,138 mi, book now 5.52, "clear near 4.42", break-even 2.82 (higher than
Kingsport's 2.50. West Coast fuel, makes sense).

**Bid 1 (fair).** 5.30 on the Vegas load. Typing in the bid box on the phone went bad: I ended up with "5.5.3030" and
the BID button greyed out, no word why. I cleared it and typed again, and the button read "BID · +2,369, CASH 1,568".
Bid in. Julia said "Yours." the moment I bid, before the round cleared. It wasn't mine yet. The clock jumped from live
pace to day-a-minute by itself. At 18:00: "Won Abilene → Las Vegas at 5.30/mi · 6,031 FD". Good.

**Sat Jan 13 (day 3).** Truck card: "Driving 2.0 h of 11 today · 14.3 h of 70 this week... Tonight, as planned: a
rest area near Williams". Williams AZ on I-40, which is exactly the way I'd run it. Somebody who's driven it built this.

**Bid 2 (high, to lose).** Long Beach → Wichita, 1,493 mi, book now 7.17, ceiling 7.82. I bid 7.60. Lost: "Lost to
7.17/mi · your bid 7.60 · your break-even 2.58. Another carrier took it outright before the round. Lost to W. Boone
Trucking at 7.17/mi". That tells me who beat me, at what, and how. Good. The rates out of LA, though: LA → Las Vegas
10.23/mi, Rancho Cucamonga → Spring Valley NV 11.52/mi, LA → Phoenix 9.21. LA outbound pays more than Vegas outbound,
that's the right shape, but nobody has paid eleven dollars a mile to Vegas since the toilet-paper spring.

**Bid 3 (low).** Henderson NV → Midland TX, 15 mi empty, book 3.97, clear near 3.70. I bid 3.40. Won it: "Won
Henderson → Midland at 3.40/mi · 3,869 FD". Henderson to Midland in 1,138 mi is long. I'd call it about a thousand.
The card says "Reload at Dallas", but Midland's 330 miles from Dallas.

**Jan 18 to 19 (days 8 and 9).** I let the clock run fast and the truck sat at Midland "idle 2 d 11 h... It has sat
out 5 bidding rounds." My fault: dispatcher off, me asleep. Same as real life. Then the Midland board: "Presidio, TX →
Conroe, TX · 13.77/mi loaded". Thirteen seventy-seven, out of Presidio. And "Andrews, TX → Corpus Christi, TX · 992 mi":
that's a 490-mile run. Somebody's map took a wrong turn.

**Bid 4 (low, homeward).** Lubbock → Fort Worth, 317 mi, 132 empty (Midland to Lubbock is about 120, right), book
7.40, clear near 6.61. I bid 6.40 and won. I set "Send home after this run · Home time · 5 days off". I told my wife
the 21st and I made Fort Worth on the 21st. The game says "You're home (Fort Worth, Dallas market)". Close enough,
Fort Worth's 30 miles.

**The settlement, Fort Worth.** "The load 2,029 · Fuel −311 · Tolls −0 · Truck upkeep −106 · The lessor's 15% −304 · The
settlement fee, 1% −20 · Held toward your own truck (kept) −609 · Yours 678 FD". It reads like a real settlement sheet.
No tolls Lubbock to Fort Worth is right, and the fuel works out to about seventy cents a mile, which is about right too. Holding
back 30% "toward your own truck" is steep, but at least it's my money and it says so.

**Fri Jan 26 (day 16), bid 5 (ceiling, to lose).** Dallas → Columbus MS, book 6.06, ceiling 6.60. I bid 6.60.
Placing the bid popped a box: "You'll go back out with 4 days at home; your days-away count won't clear." The truck
card had said "1 day off left". Which is it, four days home or one left? Lost: "That load went between rounds, to
someone who booked it." This time no name.

**Day 16, dispatcher.** Setting it up on the phone was the worst part of the session. The dispatcher card sits at the
bottom of a panel that shrinks to a two-line strip. I typed 5%, 250 mi and 12 days, switched tab, and they went back to 0,
150 and 21. No Save had been pressed and nothing warned me. In the end I ticked "Bid for me while I'm away" and saved
it on the defaults (bid at clear-near +0%, 150 mi empty, home after 21 days). Some of the trouble was the test pane
dropping taps (see the note at the end).

**Sat Feb 3 (about day 24).** The dispatcher had me in Delaware: "Delivered Cherry Hill to New Castle... 1,999 ·
Tolls −57". Tolls on that run are believable (Delaware Memorial Bridge). Then "Idle at New Castle", 8 days out, with
a button "Send to Cleveland", 400 miles empty from the I-95 corridor. No.

**A Sunday in late February (about day 45).** "Waiting at Edinburg for the dock to open Mon 07:00", in the Valley.
The run before it: "Houston to Decatur... The load 972... Yours 188 FD". 188 net for a load. A dispatcher who books
that gets fired. Then good news: "Dallas docks wave you ahead · Approved · tier 3 of 10". Shippers who know you load
you first, and that's real.

**Thu Mar 1 (day 50).** "Loaded Livingston → Rexburg · 1,078 mi... 3.15/mi", 13 days out, "1 defect", west of Salt
Lake. A Dallas man in Rexburg, Idaho, in March at 3.15 a mile, when the same game showed me 6 to 10 a mile in
January. Cash 14,109 FD (from 4,700), held toward the truck 13,946. Odometer 13,946 mi too. Same number, maybe a
coincidence. Julia: "To a sound first truck: 62,582 FD... You're 18% of the way." 13,946 of 62,582 is 22%, so
something else is being counted and the screen doesn't say what.

**Dispatcher verdict.** It runs the truck like a green dispatcher with a quota. The wheels turned: about 11,000 miles
in 34 days, around 2,250 a week, which is a solid solo week. It took me home once in the middle (I came back out "2 d out").
But it chases whatever clears near the average, anywhere in the country, with no sense of where the next load comes
from. It parked me in New Castle and suggested Cleveland. It booked a load that netted 188. It ended up in Idaho
in winter. Not a fool, but not a pro.

## 5. The three best moments

1. Losing the Long Beach load and being told straight: "Lost to W. Boone Trucking at 7.17/mi... took it outright
   before the round." It's a market with names in it.
2. The first settlement in Fort Worth: load, fuel, tolls, upkeep, lessor, net, "Paid today." I read every line,
   same as a real one.
3. "Tonight, as planned: a rest area near Williams." I've parked there.

## The five things that most got in the way

1. The rates. Once you've seen 13.77/mi, no number on the board means anything.
2. The dispatcher on a phone: a two-line strip of panel, and settings that don't stick unless you find Save.
3. Bad miles on some lanes (992 for a 490 run). If the miles are wrong, the rate per mile is wrong too.
4. Deadlines that ignore the dock hours, and nothing paid for the wait.
5. The dispatcher with no home region and no floor: Delaware, the Valley, Idaho, a 188 net.

## 6. What I never found or never understood

- How to pause. The speed button cycles Live / minute / 6 s / 2 s, and Paused only showed up after a reload or after
  pressing Space. "Press a speed or 1-4" only appears when you come back to a paused world.
- "Held toward your truck" math. Is the 30% a deposit, an escrow, or a fine, and why is it 18% of the way at 22%?
- Standing: "0.6 of the 1 counted loads tier 2 needs". Which loads count for what?
- Where the dispatcher's history is. I couldn't find a log of what it bid and lost while I was away.
- "The Reserve", "The token (later)", "Miles · your standing in the beta".
- Quick pay, factoring, brokers who pay late: "Invoices start with your own truck". Never got there.
- Whether Fort Worth home time counts as Dallas (it says "Dallas market", so I guess so).
- The test pane, not the game: taps on a fast clock got lost, the screen showed old numbers until a real Shift press,
  and two speed taps in a row only counted as one. Keys 1 to 4 and Space ran and paused the clock reliably.
  The bottom panel kept opening and closing late, after I'd already moved on.
