# Slop audit playtests (2026-11-02)

Build: https://truckingterminal.github.io/lanes-play/ as of 2026-11-01 evening, built-in browser, fresh tab, no saved world. Two sessions of about thirty minutes each. Minutes are real minutes. I did not read the code; everything here is what the screen showed.

Owner-operator start, "Kura Logistics", Dallas. Never seen the game.

**0:00** Title. Nice truck painting. **3:00** Scrolled back up. The clock read 22:00; I came in at 06:00. The ▶ button was lit the whole time: the game runs while you read. Step 1 had ticked itself off ("Look at your truck, then find your home hub on the map"). I never found Dallas; at phone size the label is four pixels tall. Now "2 of 13: Reading the board".

**4:00** Iuliia says "for now your dispatcher is you, standing beside me, holding nothing." The Fleet card under her says "sat out 3 rounds: the dispatcher's bids keep losing." Which is it? Cash 4,700 → 4,668 with no line saying why.

**5:00** Tapped LOADS. A ten-column table; six columns fit; each row is 270 px tall and mostly empty. "Dry van loads within 300 mi of Dallas," but not one leaves Dallas: 141 to 288 empty miles to every pickup. Iuliia said "bid a rate"; the board says "Book now" and "counter". **6:00** Tapped a row (Shreveport → Phoenix). A detail panel opened below it: book-now rate, my break-even, profit, "Raised 3 rounds running", a counter range. Useful, once found, and again clipped at the right ("about 105 mi empty to the n…").

## Bugs, with how to see them

1. Phone: the step-1 card art and the Dispatcher form are wider than the screen; the right third is clipped with no sideways scroll. Mobile viewport, owner-operator, read the first card and Fleet > Dispatcher.
2. Walkthrough steps complete without the player (1, 2, 4, 5, 6–10 all ticked themselves). Start any world, leave it at ▶, read.
3. Done-count runs ahead of the card: "2 of 13" heading with "3 of 13 done" under it; "5 of 13" with "6 of 13 done". Same repro.
4. Ticker keeps "Truck 2 has no driver and earns nothing" after a driver is hired. Small fleet, Staff, hire, read the bottom line.
5. "Lease another truck: No money down" fails with "Not enough FD for the lessor's deposit." Dealer, cash under the deposit.
6. Dealer "ORDER THE RIG" is enabled at 4,000 FD and does nothing on click; the used-lot buttons are grey with no reason. Owner-operator, Dealer.
7. "Used trucks: Nothing listed right now" above "The used lot: 30 on the lot this week." Dealer.
8. Step 1 describes a leased single truck to a small-fleet player.
9. Step 3 asks for an ETA the Fleet card doesn't show; step 4 asks for a receivable and due day the Money tab doesn't show.
10. Re-post (re-brokering) button on the Fleet card and in standing orders on day 6; meant to be locked until day 91.
11. Counter panel closes when a round clears while you're typing in it. Loads, open a row near 06:00 or 18:00, wait.
12. A hired driver's card shows "Truck 1" in the truck dropdown while assigned to truck 2.
13. Clicking a tab on the laptop doesn't bring its panel into view.
14. Cash −7,978 on day 42 with 270.8k revenue and 79.8k costs, and no screen that shows where it went.

The title painting. The laptop map. The load detail panel (break-even, profit, "usually clears near", counter range). "Bid in at 2.30, clears at 18:00" and "Lost at 2.30, it went for 2.12." "Truck 1: clean level 2 inspection at the Joplin scale (I-44)." The hire table. The red "Needs attention" box with a button that goes somewhere. Iuliia's lines in single doses ("Handwritten. Fountain pen.").

## The ten things that most made it feel like slop, worst first

1. **The game plays itself.** (Fleet, Loads) In an hour across two sessions I never won a load by hand; the dispatcher bid, won, re-booked and sent my trucks across the country from minute one, and the walkthrough ticked its own boxes as it went.
2. **Money vanishes with no ledger.** (Money) 270k revenue, 80k costs, −8k cash, "liquidated next week"; the tab shows no receivables, no due dates, no payroll, no fuel, nothing a trucker could argue with.
3. **Iuliia narrates a game that isn't on screen.** (The card, every tab) Wrong start, wrong clock, an ETA that isn't there, a receivable that isn't there, "your dispatcher is you" while the dispatcher is winning loads.
4. **On a phone, half the screen is chrome and the rest is clipped.** (Every screen) Logo, clock, speeds, theme, full-screen and a map eat the viewport; the card and the dispatcher form run off the right edge.
5. **Thirteen tabs, no words on the laptop, three visible on the phone.** (Tab bar) A coin, a hat, two stars; Logbook turns out to be the airdrop.
6. **The token is in the first ten minutes.** (Header, Money, Dealer, Logbook) "LAUNCH IN 730D", dollar buttons to a launch pool, an opening auction, an airdrop estimate, before I've been paid once.
7. **The load board on a phone.** (Loads) Ten columns, six fit, 270-pixel rows, no word that says "bid", and the panel disappears mid-counter when a round clears.
8. **Rates a trucker can't believe.** (Ticker, Fleet) 11.94/mi reefer, 10.24/mi van, late at those rates, "+14.1k" lines while the cash falls.
9. **The dealer is a dead end for a starter.** (Dealer) Nothing a new player can buy or lease; grey buttons with no reason; "no money down" that wants a deposit; "Nothing listed" over "30 on the lot".
10. **Thirty-four themes, a full-screen button and an eight-rule dispatcher form before a first load.** (Title, header, Fleet) The first choice the game offers a stranger is Cyber Ops versus Ivory Rose.
