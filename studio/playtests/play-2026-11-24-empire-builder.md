# Play test 2026-11-24: the empire builder (phone, 375 x 812)

Build 2ce9b53, server `lanes-player-4`, one company: "Harbor Gate TransportBig Rig Emp" (see bug 3), Los Angeles,
small-fleet start. Played from Thu Jan 11 (day 1) to Fri Feb 16 (day 37) before the call budget ran out.
Not wound up; never got past 4 trucks. The 120-day goal was not reached: most of the budget went to taps that didn't
land in the background pane (see "Test setup" at the end), not to the game.

## 1. The verdict in three lines

- I'd come back tomorrow, but annoyed: the money side is the best tycoon paperwork I've seen on a phone, and every
  "buy" sheet tells me the risk in one line. But on a phone at fast speed I couldn't hire a driver for a truck I'd
  paid for, and that truck sat for nine days.
- What limits growth isn't credit. It's the down payment against cash that drips in. The game lends you money for
  trucks but not for the cash part, so each truck costs 20k+ of savings, and two trucks + factoring brought in about
  1k FD a day.
- What the game is about: a freight carrier in a shared market. You buy and run trucks, staff them, set rules for a
  dispatcher who bids on loads for you, and live on the gap between what loads pay and what fuel, wages, upkeep and
  loans take.

## 2. Scores (1 to 10)

- **First ten minutes: 7.** Lovely title screen, a clear hub choice with real numbers, and the small-fleet start is
  exactly what I wanted. Lost points for the name field and for the hiring sheet that didn't hire.
- **Clarity: 7.** Every idle truck says why it's idle ("other carriers bid lower, stood nearer or have the better
  record"), and the hint "Your gap to the next truck is 7,398 FD" is perfect for me. But two things only came out when
  I tried them: Money/More are locked until the first load, and a loan still wants 20k cash up front.
- **Fun: 6.** Buying truck 3 with 1,719 FD left felt great. Waiting 18 days to scrape 20k for truck 4 didn't.
- **Real trucking: 8.** Hours of service, per diem, drivers on the bus to their truck, weigh stations, a reefer that
  needs a reefer load, dock standing with LA shippers. It reads like the job.
- **Cutthroat: 6.** I lose bids to other carriers and drivers vanish from the hiring list while I'm reading it. That
  is pressure. But I never saw who beat me, and nothing I did hurt anyone.
- **Looks and polish: 7.** The pixel-art title and map are lovely, and so is Julia. On a phone the panel takes the
  whole screen, and the tiny speed button and menus fight a fast clock.
- **Would I recommend it: 6.** To a tycoon friend with patience and a laptop, yes. On a phone at speed, not yet.

## 3. The diary

**Day 1, Thu Jan 11 06:00.** Title screen: a truck at dusk, a diner, NEW TEST WORLD. Gorgeous. Tapped through Julia.
Picked Los Angeles: "Freight is good here in January", 3.2 loads per truck, "hot". Insurance 25% above base,
"Security: risky". Don't care. Hot freight.

**Day 1.** Name screen. Two starts: Owner-operator, or "Small fleet. Two used trucks and 25.0k FD". Biggest stack:
small fleet. I typed "Big Rig Empire" over the suggested name. It was added to the end instead and cut off, so the
company is "Harbor Gate TransportBig Rig Emp" for good. Nothing on screen showed the name before I tapped Start.

**Day 1, 08:00.** Two trucks, a van and a reefer, both mine. Truck 2 has no driver. Hit "Hire at Los Angeles": a
staff page with ten drivers, six dispatchers, eight managers and a list of drivers to poach from rival companies
("Their employer gets 2 days to match"). That's a tycoon menu. Tapped HIRE on Carmen Diaz, 5 stars. A sheet came up:
"That's more than your company kept last week: 0 FD." Fair, it's day one. I tapped HIRE CARMEN DIAZ. Nothing. I tried
four more times while the clock ran (06:00 to 05:00 the next day). She never joined, and Tammy Bell vanished from the
list, hired by someone else. (Mostly the test pane, see the end. But a real phone player taps a fast-moving list too.)

**Day 2, Fri Jan 12.** Reloaded. The game came back PAUSED, and the speed button now says Paused. Interesting:
pausing on load is good. "Let the dispatcher bid" for truck 1. Hired Tariq Walker (5 stars, 0.93/mi) for the
reefer. The sheet was clear: about 2,604 FD a week. The Money tab was locked: "First, a load. Money, More and the
rest open the moment one is yours." I wanted to borrow first, but OK.

**Day 2, 20:00.** Truck 1 loaded, LA to Phoenix. Money opens. Payment terms: Net 30, Quick pay at 10%, or Factoring
at 5.9% for 3 months with no early exit. I pay to make things faster, so I took factoring. "Signed. Every truck,
every invoice, until Apr 13." Then I hit "Cash it now" on the one Net 30 invoice from before I signed.

**Day 2.** Finance: "Credit 60 of 100 · band C", "Truck loans: 24% down · 0.28% a week · up to 3 loans at once".
Staking needed past 5 trucks (20,000 FD). There's no cash loan, only truck loans. So I can't borrow everything.

**Day 3, Sat Jan 13.** Dealer. Used trucks are spread over the country (Chicago, Dallas, Miami, St. Louis). Cheapest
ready today: Workhorse + Dry van in St. Louis, 58.2k, "Cash needed now 23.3k FD". I had 25.1k. The confirm sheet:
"Left after · 1,755 FD · no cushion yet", "1,824 miles from home", "Worn trucks break down more... A rebuild costs
47,787 FD." Honest. Bought it. "Truck 3 has arrived. The keys are on the desk." 1,719 FD in the bank, minus 4,077
in taxes shown in red under the cash.

**Day 4-6.** Truck 3 needs a driver. I ticked "Auto-staff: fill empty seats..." expecting it to hire for me. It
didn't. Two days later truck 3 still had no driver. I hired Maria Boone (5 stars) by hand: "Maria Boone is at
Houston, so the bus here comes first: about 2 days and 65 FD, which you pay." Love that. Hired the cheapest
dispatcher, Brenda Diaz (1 star, 663/wk), because Julia said "about 33% of idle trucks sit out each round".

**Day 13, Tue Jan 23.** Cash 13,060. Staff page now says dispatch skill ★☆☆☆☆, "costs misread by ~7%". Before Brenda it
was my own ★★ and 6%. Hiring her made my bidding worse. Nobody told me before I tapped. LA docks moved me to "Approved
· tier 3 of 10 ... Your trucks go to the front of the line". Nice surprise. Julia: "Your gap to the next truck is
7,398 FD." That's the most useful line in the game for me.

**Day 16-20.** Gap down to 763 FD, then cash went sideways (19.7k to 19.6k over three days). Wages, insurance, per
diem and the dispatcher eat what the loads bring. Truck 2 idle at San Antonio, truck 3 idle at Syracuse, both
thousands of miles from home. My dispatcher's "send empty up to" rule isn't doing them any favours.

**Day 21, Wed Jan 31.** Reloaded to get the screen to hold still. Julia had a new lesson: weigh stations, "conditional
rating", "Tolstoy had his estate; I have charts." Truck 1 shows "1 defect". Ordered a used-lot Workhorse, 44.3k,
40% condition, on a loan: 20,066 FD now, 386 FD a week for 104 weeks, plus a 3,035 FD "loan stake, locked until the
loan is repaid". Cash: 676 FD. Ready Feb 7. Empire mode.

**Day 28, Wed Feb 7.** "Truck 4 has arrived." Cash back to 6,159 FD. Hired nobody, because I couldn't get a tap to
land at 2-second days: the hiring list redraws under my finger.

**Day 31, Sat Feb 10.** "Truck 4 has been idle 3 d at Los Angeles: No driver is seated, so it can't run." Truck 1 (me)
and Maria are both "heading home for days off" after 22-23 days out. My one paid-off-ish truck goes home to rest.

**Day 37, Fri Feb 16.** Stopped here. Cash 5,139 FD, 4 trucks, 3 drivers (counting me), one 1-star dispatcher, two
truck loans (3 allowed), factoring to Apr 13. Truck 4 still has no driver, nine days after it arrived. The Staff
page offers "DRIVE IT" for me to take truck 4 myself, which I only saw on the last screen. Julia's line: "Past 5
trucks you need a yard." I'm a long way from ten trucks.

### What limited my growth, in the order I hit it

1. **Day 1, drivers:** "No driver: the truck can't run." / "Truck 2 has no driver and earns nothing." I spent the
   first game day not managing to hire.
2. **Day 2, the gate on the first load:** "First, a load. Money, More and the rest open the moment one is yours."
3. **Day 2, credit is for trucks only:** "Truck loans: 24% down · 0.28% a week · up to 3 loans at once" and no cash
   loan anywhere.
4. **Day 2, dispatch losing bids:** "44 dry van loads in range pay enough, but the dispatcher's bids haven't won:
   other carriers bid lower, stood nearer or have the better record."
5. **Day 3, cash for the down payment:** "Cash needed now 23.3k FD" with 25.1k; then "Left after · 1,755 FD".
6. **Day 4, dispatch coverage:** "Your dispatch team covers 2 trucks and you run 3: about 33% of idle trucks sit out
   each round."
7. **Days 13-21, the slow cash drip:** "Your gap to the next truck is 7,398 FD" ... "763 FD", then cash flat for days.
8. **Day 21, the factory and the lot:** "New trucks are ready 37 days after you order them: the factory is behind";
   the used lot "ready Feb 7".
9. **Day 28-37, attention (hiring at speed):** "Truck 4 has been idle 3 d at Los Angeles: No driver is seated".
10. **Ahead, seen but not hit:** "You may run 5: your yard holds 5", "Staking: needed past 5 trucks (20,000 FD for
    the next tier)", "Past 7 trucks you need an operations manager", "A maintenance manager past 4", "Past 5 trucks
    you need a yard".

So the real order: **attention first, cash second, then the rules wall at 5 and 7.** Credit was never the limit: I
only used 2 of my 3 loans because I never had the cash for the third down payment. The market was never the limit
either (LA had 40-55 loads posted).

## 5. The three best moments

1. Buying truck 3 on day 3. The sheet said exactly what I was risking ("Left after 1,755 FD", "1,824 miles from home",
   "A rebuild costs 47,787 FD") and let me do it anyway. Then: "The keys are on the desk."
2. "Maria Boone is at Houston, so the bus here comes first: about 2 days and 65 FD, which you pay." A tiny detail
   that makes the world real.
3. "Los Angeles docks wave you ahead · Approved · tier 3 of 10". A reward I didn't know existed.

## The five things that most got in the way

1. Hiring at speed on a phone: the list moves, the tap misses, the truck sits.
2. Auto-staff didn't fill the seats I bought trucks for.
3. No cash loan. The tycoon in me wants a credit line; the game only finances the 76% of a truck.
4. The cheap dispatcher made me worse, and nothing said so until I read the Staff page.
5. The speed button. It cycles Paused → Live → Day a minute → 6 s → 2 s with one small button, and the menu's own
   speed buttons were off-screen when I tried them.

## 6. What I never found or never understood

- What the "loan stake" is for, and whether I get it back with interest ("locked until the loan is repaid").
- Whether auto-staff was on. The checkbox had no visible state I could find on the phone.
- Who beat my bids. "Other carriers bid lower": which ones, by how much?
- The Rates, Intel, Standing, Economy and Business screens (all marked New). I never had the spare taps.
- What "0.81× the reload run priced in" means.
- Why my dispatcher sent trucks to Syracuse and San Antonio with "head for home after" set.
- What the factor does if a shipper doesn't pay.
- "Miles · your standing in the beta" and "The token (later)" on the Money page. It looks like crypto talk in a
  trucking game. I skipped it.

## Test setup (not the game)

- The pane ran in the background the whole time. Screens and `find` results lagged one frame behind; a real Shift
  press before every read fixed it. Clicks by element landed in the right place only when the panel had not
  redrawn in between; at "a game day every 2 s" the panel redraws several times a second, so many taps missed.
  Reloading the page pauses the world, and that was the only reliable way to act.
- About 60 of my ~135 tool calls went on taps that didn't land. That is why I stopped at day 37, not 120.
- Coordinates: the screenshot reports a 750 x 1624 frame, but clicks use the 375 x 812 image pixels.

## 7. Then and now

- **Gone:** the hire tap that hired twice. Every hire now has a confirm sheet with the weekly cost and "the one
  risk". The clipped Dealer buttons and the "$2,885 (TO THE LAUNCH POOL)" pseudo-button were not on my phone Dealer.
- **Gone:** the "with tax costs less than asking" line. I started without a lease, so I couldn't recheck the netted deposit.
- **Still there:** the hiring list shifts under your finger. Then it hired twice; now the tap or the sheet just misses.
- **Still there:** the 3-loan cap at band C, and used prices swinging (a Workhorse reefer asked 147.5k on day 3; its
  like asked 81.2k on day 21).
- **New and better:** the "Your gap to the next truck is N FD" hint, the idle-truck reasons, the driver's bus ride and
  dock standing tiers. The world also pauses on reload, so a player can stop and act.
- **New and better:** the buy sheet now spells out "Left after", distance from home and the rebuild cost.
- **New and worse:** a "loan stake" (3-4k FD locked) on top of the down payment, which made cash, not credit, my
  limit. And a 1-star dispatcher quietly lowers your dispatch skill.
- **Not tested:** repossession, the month report and missed payments. I never got far enough into debt or time.
