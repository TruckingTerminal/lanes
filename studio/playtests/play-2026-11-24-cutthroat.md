# LANES play test, 2026-11-24: the cutthroat (laptop)

Build 2ce9b53 on my own copy (`lanes-player-2`, localhost:4182), built-in browser at its own laptop size (the page
reports a 1024 x 768 viewport). Small-fleet start in Los Angeles. I played from Thu Jan 11 to Fri Mar 16, Year 1:
**65 game days**, mostly at "Day in 6 s" and "Day in 2 s". 123 tool calls, 17 screenshots.

## 1. The verdict

- I'd come back tomorrow, but to make money, not for the knife fight. The trucking is real and hard (Net 30 nearly
  sank me twice). "Cutthroat" is mostly talk for now: every dirty trick I tried was blocked cleanly, cost me, or
  wasn't possible.
- The only way I found to hurt another company was to poach its driver, and the rival matched in two days. The
  player-vs-player part (the Back Lot) is marked "opens with a later beta".
- What the game is about: you run a small trucking company in one shared market. You win loads on a board, you
  haul them on real roads, and you live or die on the gap between when you spend and when shippers pay you.

## 3. The diary

**Thu Jan 11, 06:00. Title screen.** A truck at dusk in Monument Valley, one button: NEW TEST WORLD. Classy. I
clicked it and got "Starting the world…" for about 5 seconds.

**Jan 11. Julia.** "I've helped start more carriers than I've had birthdays." Fine. The first NEXT click did
nothing; the second advanced. Three hubs offered: LA, Newark, Chicago. LA says "crowded, and dear to insure",
"Security: risky. Cargo goes missing here often." Crowded means rivals to fight, so I picked LA.

**Jan 11. Company name.** The field already held "Harbor Gate Transport". I typed "Lowball Freight" and the game
saved "Harbor Gate TransportLowball Fre" (glued and cut off). My fault for not clearing it, but nothing showed me
that the name was pre-filled text. I took the **Small fleet** start (two used trucks, a van and a reefer, 25.0k FD)
because a ruthless player takes the bigger stack.

**Jan 11. First look.** Under each truck there's a SCRAP button: "Scrap · 4,200 FD (worth 94.8K)", right beside
SELL. One mis-tap destroys 90k of value. The game also says my 94.8k truck would only fetch 57–76k from fleets.
Noted.

**Jan 11. The board.** 49 dry van loads out of LA, each with total pay, rate, empty miles and profit. I sorted by
eye and went for the fattest: **San Bernardino → Arlington, 1,525 mi, 11,090 FD, +7,665 profit**. The load card is
excellent: break-even 2.20/mi, "loads like it clear near 7.06/mi", a ceiling of 7.93, a no-show claim of 2,773, a
late claim of 2,218, and "67 h to spare". Undercutting is pointless here: BOOK NOW at 7.27 is a sure price, so
bidding under it just gives money away. I booked it. The truck card then offered "RELEASE · 1,109 FD", so backing
out costs 10%.

**Jan 11. Hiring.** Truck 2 had no driver. The hire table has a pay box on every row, so I typed 0.55 into Dale
Reyes's box (a 4-star driver asking 0.84). Then the table shifted under my mouse and my click landed on
"OFFER 1.04 FD/MI" for **Tammy Reyes**, a 5-star driver at Cottonwood Hills Logistics. The ticker said: "Tammy
Reyes accepted your offer; their employer has 2 days to match it." I poached by accident! The Dale dialog that
popped up quoted "about 2,338 FD a week", his full ask, so my 0.55 was ignored. I said Not now and waited on
Tammy.

**Jan 11. The second-tab trick.** I opened the game in a second tab, clicked Continue and tried to RELEASE the
Arlington load from there. A yellow bar: "Your world is open in another tab; this one only watches." Clean
block, no double-dealing. I closed it.

**Jan 11. Rivals.** In More, then Intel: 581 companies, ranked by Miles. I'm #528. The top is Los Angeles Cartage, 6
trucks, in my own hub. "Lost loads this week: Los Angeles lost 55 loads; rates there are up 48%." I can see them,
but there's nothing to click to touch them. The Back Lot says: "A later beta: player against player. A gate and
cameras for your yard". Good tease. Not yet.

**Jan 11. Dispatcher.** I set "bid at what loads usually clear near, plus" to **−15** (undercut everyone), empty
miles up to 300 and home after 28 days, then pressed SAVE ("Dispatcher saved."). The "Bid for me" switch looked
unchecked after every click; I couldn't tell its state.

**Sun Jan 14.** Tammy declined: "stays with Cottonwood Hills Logistics." The rival matched. Truck 2 sat idle for 3
days waiting on her. **The poach cost me 3 days of a reefer.** I hired the cheapest driver, Yusuf Diaz (1 star,
0.67), and tried the lowball in his pay box again: the dialog quoted 1,865 a week, his ask, and he was hired at
0.67.

**Jan 14. The pay cut.** Straight after hiring him I hit SET PAY with 0.40. Tooltip: "Below the pay floor: 0.54
FD/mi, 85% of their market rate." 0.54 was refused too; **0.55 went through**. Yusuf went from "happy" to
"settled" and kept driving for two months without complaint. The ruthless move pays a little.

**Jan 25.** First settlement card: "Delivered Alamo to Chicago … Yours 7,597 FD … Due Feb 23." So I'm not paid
for a month. The dispatcher was winning loads at 3.85 and 4.05/mi, far below my 7.27 opener. Rates are falling,
as the city card warned.

**Jan 26.** Cash was 10,700, down from 24,700, with everything owed to me stuck in Net 30.

**Sun Feb 4.** **Cash −986 FD.** "You're in the red. 4 weekly checks in a row more than 2,000 FD under and the
company is wound up." The same screen said "Your first month: earned 56,525, spent 20,876, kept 35,649 FD." Kept
but not cash. Every invoice had a CASH IT NOW button (about 10% fee). As a cutthroat I let it ride, because the
Arlington money landed in 3 days. It worked.

**Feb.** Shipper standing climbed in Savannah, Detroit, Memphis, Chicago and Ontario. "Dallas docks wave you ahead:
tier 3 of 10." That was nice: being on time 100% has a payoff I can see.

**Fri Mar 2.** The February statement: kept 10,354 FD, 15 loads, 100% on time, 66% of miles loaded. Cash 38,728.
The "Kept isn't cash" breakdown is the clearest money screen I've seen in any game.

**Mar 2. Borrow and stop paying.** In the Dealer's used lot I ordered a worn Workhorse and dry van **on a loan**:
14.8k down, 411 FD a week for 104 weeks, "First payment Mar 4, and every week after, even while the truck waits at
the dealer." In orange: "**Miss 2 payments and the lender takes the truck.**" The confirm dialog said "Under 4
weeks of cushion after this" and "Worn trucks break down more". Nowhere did it say *you have no driver for it*.
There's no button to skip a payment; payments just come out. Getting the order through took three tries: the first
confirm click didn't land, and the screenshot kept showing my old cash.

**Mar 9–16.** Truck 3 arrived in LA and sat: "idle 7 d … No driver is seated, so it can't run." The third truck
also overloaded my dispatching: "2 trucks' worth of attention for the 3 you run, so this truck sits out about 33%
of rounds." That's a rule I never saw coming, and now truck 1 sits too. Cash fell to **−3,072 FD**. "The weekly
check is Mar 18. At today's cash you'd be under the line … four in a row … wind the company up on Sun Apr 8."
Loan payments, an idle new truck and a stretched dispatcher are stacking up.

**Fri Mar 16, day 65. Stopped.** I looked at the dispatcher panel and it read **+0** and **150 mi**, not my −15
and 300. Only the 28 days had stuck. Either the game clamped my undercut without a word or the save never took. (I
filled those fields with the browser tool's form fill, not keystrokes, so this may be how I entered them; the
game still said "Dispatcher saved.") I was 26 days out without home time, and Yusuf was 23 days out. Julia warns
"A tired driver crashes more", but nothing bad had happened yet.

**How it felt:** the game punishes sloppiness (the cash line, idle trucks, release fees) more than it rewards
ruthlessness. I never had an enemy. The market was the enemy.

## 5. The three best moments

1. The **load card**: break-even, clearing rate, ceiling, profit, the exact claim for a no-show and for a late
   delivery, and "67 h to spare". It tells you what you're signing.
2. **"Tammy Reyes declined: stays with Cottonwood Hills Logistics."** The one time a rival pushed back. That's the
   cutthroat game I came for.
3. **"Kept isn't cash: what else moved it"** on the monthly statement. It finally explained why I was broke after
   "keeping" 35k.

## The five things that most got in the way

1. Clicks, pause and screenshots going stale until a Shift press. I never trusted that the game had heard me.
2. The hire table overflowing at laptop size and the accidental poach.
3. Dispatcher numbers that didn't stick, and a switch whose state I couldn't read.
4. Nobody to fight: rivals are a leaderboard, and the Back Lot is "later".
5. Hidden knock-on rules when you grow (dispatch coverage, driverless loan trucks) that only show up after they
   cost you.

## 6. What I never found or never understood

- How to deliver late on purpose: there's no control for it.
- Whether insurance can be dropped or cut.
- The **Reserve** ("Cash and the Reserve together are 1,137 FD under the line"). I never found what it is or how to
  put money in.
- **Miles** ("17,655 Miles · your standing in the beta") and the token. What do Miles buy?
- The Rates, Economy and Business tabs opened during play (Business on Jan 29); I never opened them.
- "Book loads between rounds at break-even plus" in More rules, versus the main bid percentage.
- Whether the dispatcher switch was on. It won loads, so it must have been, but the box looked empty.
- Book value (94.8k) versus what fleets pay (57–76k): which one counts toward anything?

## 7. Then and now

Against the exploit hunter's diary of 2026-11-10 (build c8c67db):

- **Gone:** the two-tab save-scum. The second tab now says "Your world is open in another tab; this one only watches."
- **Gone:** February's statement missing a line. Mine has "Invoices not yet paid −32,253" and "Earlier invoices paid this
  month +56,525", and it adds up to the cash at the close.
- **Better:** the loan's second confirm now opens on screen at laptop size (though my first click on it still didn't
  land). A booked load can be released, for a 10% fee shown up front. Driver pay has a floor (85% of market).
- **Still there:** the Staff list shifting under the cursor. It cost me an accidental poach this time. PAUSED's state is
  still hard to read and didn't always hold.
- **Still there, changed:** the name field. Then a blank name was silently swapped for "Harbor Gate Transport". Now that
  name is pre-filled, and typing appends to it.
- **New and better:** poaching from rivals, with the employer's 2-day match. "Kept isn't cash." Shipper standing tiers
  ("Dallas docks wave you ahead").
- **New and worse:** the hire table now overflows the side panel sideways instead of wrapping names. Dispatcher settings
  I saved came back changed. Growing to a third truck quietly cuts dispatch coverage to 67% of rounds.
