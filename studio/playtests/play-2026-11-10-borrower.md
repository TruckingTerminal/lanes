# Play diary: the borrower

- **Who I was:** someone who grows on debt as fast as the game lets them: the first truck on the smallest cash, then a
  second and a third, drivers, a dispatcher, quick pay whenever it's offered. I rode into a cash crunch on purpose.
- **Build:** lanes-play `87864b2` (Deploy c8c67db), version.json `{"build":"c8c67db"}`, ruleset 0.24.0-phase0.41. No
  newer build between sessions.
- **Screen and speed:** phone (375 × 812) up to the first truck, then laptop (1100 × 700). "Day in 2 s" and "Day in 6 s"
  between decisions.
- **How long:** about 45 real minutes, Thu Jan 11 to Sun Jun 10, Year 1 (game day ~151).
- **Where I ended:** Lakeshore Leverage Freight, Chicago. I had three owned trucks on three loans; the lender took two.
  Now one used reefer (driver Hung Mills), one driver with no truck (Anjali Ruiz), two dispatchers I didn't mean to
  hire, 6,317 FD in cash and 31,296 FD owed.
- **Fresh world:** ≡ → Title screen → NEW TEST WORLD → REPLACE IT. The title reloads the page now, and nothing carried
  over from the old world: no cards, no badges. Last round's leak looks fixed.
- **Screenshots:** in [play-2026-11-10-borrower/](play-2026-11-10-borrower/).

## The diary

**Day 1 (Jan 11), Chicago.** "9 loads posted last round · 2.69/mi average · 0.7 loads per truck". What now?: "Book Chicago
to Orlando. 565 over break-even, plus your 917 draw as the driver: 508 to cash now, 973 toward your truck." The load page
says the same ("Cash now 508 FD · includes your 917 FD draw"), so last round's two different cash numbers agree now.
It's still a first suggestion into Florida ("Reload at Orlando: poor"). Ceiling 3.52 above book-now 2.66, yet BID is
disabled: "I wouldn't bid on this one: loads like it usually clear at or under Book now." Booked.

**Day 1, looking for credit.** Money → "Loans" leads to a Finance teaser. A leased starter can't borrow anything until
the first truck. Fair enough.

**Day 32 (~Feb 12), "You can buy today".** Iuliia's page: "Next on the road: You can buy today. I'd keep about 12,000 FD
back for fuel and repairs before you sign." Cash 9,111. The Dealer now opens by itself (DealerNew). Its first card, word
for word: "You can order today · ready May 9. That's for the truck that takes the least cash today: a used Workhorse
with a dry van, 450k miles, 40% condition, ready May 9. Or a truck with a dry van, ready today: 6,548 FD in cash. The
truck I've priced for your first is worn, at 40% condition … I'd wait for a sounder one. I'd keep about 12,000 FD back."
Three problems:
- No listing on the page needs 6,548 FD. The cheapest ready-today truck needs 10.5k cash.
- "Asking 69.7k FD … It costs 64.5k FD with tax." With tax it costs less than the asking price. That's the 10.1k lease
  deposit netted in, but the line doesn't say so.
- Iuliia says buy today, wait for a sounder one, and keep 12k back, all on one card.

**Day 34, the smallest cash.** A lot rig on a loan: "Your lease deposit 10.1k FD, counted toward the price · Cash needed now
4,026 FD". The confirm was clear: "ready May 9, and handed over at the first day's end after that with your leased truck
idle. Your lease ends then." ORDER · 4,026 FD. 86 days' wait for a used truck off a lot. On the phone the new-rig button
reads "RDER THE RIG (REPLACES YOUR LEASE) · 192.3", cut off at both ends. Under it is "OR $2,885 (TO THE LAUNCH POOL)",
a real-dollar price styled like a button on the Dealer ([01](play-2026-11-10-borrower/01-dealer-dollar-button-clipped.jpg)).

**Day 40–120, paying for a truck I don't have.** Money: "Loans: 39,469 FD owed, for 1 truck on order · 505 FD a week".
Payments started the day I ordered, and I paid twelve weeks (about 6,000 FD) on a truck still on the lot. The confirm never
said payments start before the truck arrives. On ~Apr 22 the Dealer said "Your truck is ready May 9, in 17 days" and
called my leased truck "truck 888" ("A truck changes hands only while your leased one stands idle, and truck 888 is on
a run to Jacksonville now"). Everywhere else it's Truck 1. Used prices that day: "Asking 115.5k FD (value 210.3k)",
"Asking 85.6k (value 155.7k)". On May 12 the same lot showed "Asking 11.4k FD (value 36.3k)" and a "100% condition"
Highway Pro reefer at 36.7k ([06](play-2026-11-10-borrower/06-dealer-cheap-used.jpg)). Prices and values swing by
half within three weeks. New trucks: "ready 77 days after you order", then 52, then 41.

**Day 121 (Sat May 12), the handover.** It came three days late, because the dispatcher had the leased truck "Running
empty to El Paso", so it was never idle. Then "New truck delivered: truck 1 is ready", worth 49,632. "SEND HOME AFTER
THIS RUN · HOME TIME · 5 DAYS OFF" now shows on a moving truck. Last round's blocker is fixed.

**Day 121, borrowing to the cap.** BUY ON A LOAN on two used reefers (Atlanta 36.7k, Memphis 37.3k), about 10.8k cash
each. The confirms are good: "33% of its life left, no rebuilds … It runs until it's worn out, then goes for scrap, for
about 5,310 FD." The third, an 11.4k dry van, was refused with a quiet line under the button: "The lender carries at
most 3 loans at your credit." Clear, if easy to miss.

**Day 121, staff.** HIRE on Anjali Ruiz (★★★★★) hired her with one tap. In session 1 a confirm sheet came first; here
none did. Seconds after hiring, her card said "14 d out · fatigue 57%". Hung Mills, the same. Then HIRE on the cheapest
dispatcher (674/wk). The list shifted under my second tap and I'd hired two dispatchers: Nabil Nguyen and Sanjay
Duarte, 1,355 FD a week together, for three trucks that one could cover
([04](play-2026-11-10-borrower/04-second-dispatcher-hired.jpg)). On the laptop the staff table is cut off at the left
("an / oreno") ([05](play-2026-11-10-borrower/05-staff-table-cut.jpg)).

**Day 127, quick pay.** Cash −6,557. "Loans: 99,489 FD owed · 1,283 FD a week". CASH IT NOW on Santa Fe → Hamilton: "Fee ·
8.0% spot + 0.1% at 1 days old · 556 FD · You get today · 6,303 FD". Cash −255. Then I stopped, on purpose.

**Day 133, the crunch.** Cash −14,898. Money: "You're in the red … 1 of 4 weekly checks in debt; the next is Sun May 27."
Credit: "Credit 62 of 100 · band C", and on all three loans, "1 payment missed. 2 in all and the lender takes the truck."
([02](play-2026-11-10-borrower/02-missed-payments-credit.jpg)). What now? said: "Cash the invoices due by Jun 21. 1 of 4
weekly checks in debt. The next is May 27. 14,127 FD lands today". That's good advice, but it only speaks of the
four-week clock, and the real danger was one week away: the next missed payment takes the trucks.

**Day 141, the lender takes two trucks.** A week later two trucks were gone. Nothing in the Today log says so: no "the
lender took truck 2", nothing. The surviving reefer was renumbered "Truck 1". Loans went from 99,764 to 31,605 owed, and
cash from −14,898 to +10,794. Anjali Ruiz is now "no truck" and still on the payroll. May's report
([03](play-2026-11-10-borrower/03-may-report.jpg)) has "Loans taken +94,412 · Loan principal repaid −1,712", and no
line for the two trucks taken or the ~68,000 of loans that left with them. It also reads "27 loads delivered · 59% on
time · 18,741 of 435,579 miles loaded (4%)": the odometers of the used trucks I bought seem to count as May's miles. The
forecast line under it is excellent: "At this rate your cash goes below zero around Thu Jun 8, as low as −9,870 FD,
before your next invoice lands on Tue Jun 13".

**Day 151.** It survived with one truck, 6,317 FD, 31,296 owed. No wind-up, and plenty left to play.

## The five things that most got in the way
1. Trucks repossessed without a word.
2. A month report that can't explain my own cash.
3. What now? watching the wrong clock in a crunch.
4. Paying a loan for three months on a truck I couldn't drive.
5. Hire taps that hire twice.

## The three best moments
1. The loan sheet and the "lender carries at most 3 loans" cap. It's honest about limits.
2. The cash forecast: "your cash goes below zero around Thu Jun 8 … before your next invoice lands on Tue Jun 13".
3. Send home on a moving truck, finally.

## What I did not understand
- What happened to my two trucks and the money they owed.
- Why my own driver card disappeared (I was the driver of truck 1) and whether I still drive.
- Why used trucks were worth 210k one month and 36k the next.

## Ugly or broken
Clipped Dealer buttons on the phone; the staff table cut off on laptop.

## Would I come back tomorrow?
Yes. Riding debt is tense and the warnings mostly land. But the silent repossession would feel like the game cheated
me.

## What the game is about, as I understood it
Credit is a tool with teeth: three loans at most, two missed payments and the truck is gone, and net-30 means growth
eats cash before it makes any. The rules are sound. The game just doesn't tell you when it uses them on you.
