# Player round 2026-11-24: summary

Build 2ce9b53 (ruleset 0.25.6-phase0.49) on the test page. Five personas, each a fresh player on its own copy of the
deployed site, playing blind. Each diary ends with a "Then and now" against a diary from the last rounds (2026-11-10,
2026-11-03). The question for the round: is what the studio built in the last two weeks any good?

**The short answer.** What was fixed stayed fixed, the art and the trucking detail are liked by everyone, and the
game underneath is not yet fun enough to bring most players back. Two of five would come back tomorrow, one maybe,
two would not. Fun and "would recommend" average 5 of 10. "Feels cutthroat" averages 4.

## The ten worst, by how many players hit them and how hard

1. **The dispatcher plays the game, and on its defaults plays it badly.** After the first load an owner-operator has
   nothing left to decide. The stranger's dispatcher took 1,153 miles at 1.86/mi for −17 FD (More rules: "the eased
   floor can go as far as 12% under break-even"). The trucker's netted 188 FD on a load, parked in New Castle with
   "Send to Cleveland", and was in Rexburg at 3.15/mi on day 50. The builder's trucks idled in Syracuse and San
   Antonio. No home region, no minimum net, no log of what it bid and lost.
2. **Nobody to fight.** Intel lists 581 companies and they can only be looked at. The one move against a rival is a
   driver poach, which the rival matched. The Back Lot reads "a later beta". The cutthroat: "I never had an enemy.
   The market was the enemy."
3. **The goals are far off.** A first truck is "about 59 loads away … around four months". A brokerage needs about
   93,000 FD of carrier cash with no loan for it and a 2% weekly holding cost against the saving: 14 game months
   from the owner-operator start. The builder's next truck was 18 days of drip.
4. **Rates a trucker won't believe.** 13.77/mi and 11.52/mi on the same board as 1.84/mi in the opening weeks.
   "Once you've seen 13.77/mi, no number on the board means anything."
5. **Two suspected rule bugs (to confirm).** Lane miles about double on some lanes ("Andrews, TX → Corpus Christi,
   TX · 992 mi"); a delivery deadline before the receiver opens ("Deliver by Sun Jan 14 16:28", "Receiver opens Mon
   Jan 15 07:00 · about 49 h waiting").
6. **The company name box.** It comes filled in; typing adds to it and the result is cut at 32 characters with no
   sign. Four of five companies got a mangled name; the fifth was warned. A brokerage name typed twice never took.
7. **The dispatcher's settings.** On a phone they sit in a panel that shrinks to a strip. The on/off switch saves at
   once and wipes typed fields. Edits are lost without Save and nothing warns. The switch's state can't be read.
8. **Hiring.** At 1024 wide the table overflows its panel and rows move under the pointer (an accidental poach; a
   paid-for truck nine days without a driver). Auto-staff, ticked, filled no seat. A one-star dispatcher lowers the
   company's dispatch skill and the hire sheet doesn't say. A truck bought with no driver for it gets no warning.
9. **Numbers that disagree.** The board chip "+1,285" against "Yours 937"; three prices for a first truck (Dealer
   80,300, Help "a quarter of 182,000", Julia "64,472 to go"); "18% of the way" at 13,946 of 62,582; Help's 4,700
   against the statement's 5,000; "1 day off left" against "4 days at home".
10. **Words from outside trucking.** "NEW TEST WORLD", "The token (later)", "Miles · your standing in the beta",
    "The Reserve"; FD is never explained. Two players read the Money tab as a crypto pitch.

Also named: guide step 7 ("Compare revenue against costs on Money") has nothing to compare until the month closes;
Julia says "Yours." before a bid clears; the broker's bond is an unspoken cap ("74,954 of your 75,000 FD bond in
use" in three weeks) and a 15% margin fills at once against "Brokers here take 7%–12%"; the bid box adds to what is
in it; the Rates table and a city card are cut off at phone width; "Ways out" buttons vanish when the dispatcher acts
first; SCRAP sits beside SELL.

## What got better since the last rounds (each re-checked by a player)

- A second tab can't overwrite the world: "Your world is open in another tab; this one only watches."
- The monthly statement adds up, and "Kept isn't cash" explains the gap.
- A lost bid names the winner and the rate: "Lost to W. Boone Trucking at 7.17/mi".
- Julia names the dispatcher right after the first load; the 70-hour clock, the night's stop and dock hours show
  before they bite.
- The cash forecast on a load is close to the payout; the settlement has a tolls line that reads right.
- Every hire and every purchase has a confirm sheet that names the cost and the one risk.
- A brokerage can no longer run deep into minus, and its margin screen gives a yardstick.

## What players liked most

The title screen and the map (all five); the first booking, when the clock speeds up and the truck drives off; the
load card (break-even, clearing rate, ceiling, the claims, hours to spare); the settlement sheet and the month
report; "docks wave you ahead · tier 3 of 10" (four of five, unprompted); the brokerage Money screen; "Maria Boone
is at Houston, so the bus here comes first"; "Tonight, as planned: a rest area near Williams".

## Part two: the empire builder to day 143

The empire builder's first session stopped at day 37, so the same company was played on to Sat Jun 2 (day 143). It
re-scored clarity 6 (from 7), cutthroat 7 (from 6) and would recommend 5 (from 6).

- **Peak five trucks, ended with three.** A breakdown on payment day (−5,045 FD) put cash at −8,187 FD and all three
  loans one payment behind. The lender took three trucks over the next nine weeks.
- **The debt warning is the clearest screen in the game:** "You're in debt at a weekly check: 1 of 4" and "the
  company is wound up on Sun Apr 22". The "way out" under it names one 532 FD payment while 1,309 FD a week is due.
- **Repossession now has a voice** (the 11-10 borrower lost trucks "without a word"): a full sheet, "The lender took
  Truck 4", and a line in the month report. The sheet points to "today's news" for the figures, and the player found
  no news screen; cash moved from −8,187 to +25,249 FD with no line saying why at the time.
- **The Finance page and the trucks disagreed for weeks** after the first repossession. With engineering.
- **The wall at five trucks is the real ceiling:** "A small yard costs 79,966 FD plus a bond of 30,000 FD", and a
  20,000 FD stake that by itself opens nothing ("You may run 5: your yard holds 5 … Staking allows 10"), against a
  company keeping 13,000 to 21,000 FD a month. The price shows only on "More yard space".
- **Auto-staff read "on" and seated nobody:** a truck stood 49 days without a driver while a hired driver was "Paid
  with no truck: about 810 FD a week".
- **Prices in dollars beside prices in FD** on the Dealer and Facilities ("10,091 FD / OR $2,717", "Or $675") read
  as pay-to-win.
- Smaller: a five-star driver hired at "fatigue 100%" went straight home; a stake has no confirm sheet; "company
  truck cap" beside "The company limit is 30"; a bus "about 4 days" arriving in eleven; a reward sheet cut off at
  phone width.

The part-two diary is filed after engineering's look at what it found.

## Not covered

## The test rig

The built-in browser pane ran in the background. Taps on a fast clock were dropped and screenshots lagged until a
real Shift press. Players who paused before tapping (Space, or a reload) lost less. A setting filled with the
browser tool's form fill may not be saved by the game, so the cutthroat's "saved −15%, read back +0%" needs a check
by hand before it counts as a bug.
