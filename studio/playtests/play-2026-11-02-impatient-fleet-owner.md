# Play diary: an impatient fleet owner's first month

- **Who I was:** a fleet owner on a phone who skims. I never read a paragraph, I tap the first thing that looks like
  progress, and I expect the game to keep up.
- **Build:** lanes-play `adfb916` ("Deploy 156ca71 from lanes", 2026-11-03 00:37 UTC). A small deploy landed after
  session 1. I pulled before playing as the role says, so this is one build newer than session 1.
- **Setup:** my own copy on a fresh port (4181), so session 1's world on 4180 is untouched. Phone, 375 × 812. Day a
  minute, with one burst at "Day in 6 s" in the middle (Jan 11 to 14), because this man would crank it.
- **How long:** 27 real minutes, Wed Jan 3 to Fri Feb 2, Year 1. Minutes below are real minutes from opening the page.
- **Where I ended:** Harbor Gate Transport, Los Angeles. Three trucks running and two on order (arriving Feb 28 and
  Mar 20), 33 loads delivered, 67% on time, #131 of 521 carriers, 59,538 FD in cash, 76,156 FD in loans, "net worth
  340.3k". I picked zero loads all month. Paused.
- **Screenshots:** in [play-2026-11-02-impatient-fleet-owner/](play-2026-11-02-impatient-fleet-owner/).

## The diary

**Min 0 · title.** Tapped NEW TEST WORLD. "Starting the world…" showed at once
([01](play-2026-11-02-impatient-fleet-owner/01-starting-the-world.jpg)). That's new since session 1, where I sat through

HERE shimmers, so I tapped it.

"Small fleet. Two used trucks and 25.0k FD", and started.

**Min 1 · Jan 3, 08:00 · in.** The clock was already running at Day a minute. A red "Needs you: Truck 2 has no driver

([03](play-2026-11-02-impatient-fleet-owner/03-first-screen-needs-you.jpg)). Two game hours in, the dispatcher had

## The five things that most got in the way

Ranked by how a stranger would feel them.

1. **Big money goes in one tap, with no warning and no explanation.** I bought a truck at 85% of my cash, down to 3.7k,
   and only then learned it's past its last rebuild with 15% of its life left. An APU took 23.5k and I still don't know
   what it is. Hire, quick pay and scrap are all one tap. A loan, though, gets a full confirm card. Guardrails sit on the
   safe actions and not on the dangerous ones, and a skimmer finds every dangerous one.
2. **Every way to grow is locked or misleading.** "Used trucks deliver today", but the used lot arrives in 6 to 10 weeks,
   after you've paid. "No money down", but "not enough for the lessor's deposit", with no amount. BORROW offers a figure
   and answers "Over the loan-to-value limit". MORE YARD SPACE goes to a locked page. The yard limit appears only after
   the second order. By week four I had 78k and nothing to buy that helped the fleet. The fleet start promises a fleet
   game that month one won't let you play.
3. **The game doesn't need me.** The dispatcher bids from minute one ("You won one" before I'd touched anything),
   Auto-staff hires a driver and an 843-a-week dispatcher when I'm broke, and buttons vanish under my thumb when a round
   fires. I chose zero loads and 33 were delivered. The two things I can do are spend and wait, and spending is a trap
   (1 and 2).
4. **On a phone, the controls that matter are hidden or move.** The fleet choice sits under the START button. HIRE
   buttons are off the right edge. Finance cards jump when tapped, so the first tap lands on the wrong one. The sheet
   jumps to full height mid-tap. The month close and its READ THE MONTH button last about seven seconds.
5. **The messages contradict each other.** "Paid Feb 5" while I'm on Quick pay. "The lessor's cut" on trucks I own.
   "Kept 114,733" next to 59k cash. "Fines paid 0" after three tickets. A reused invoice amount. Red "Late" every night.
   Tired-driver cards with no action. Two loan rates on one page. Breakdowns as grey text while "Rates · New" gets the
   same red badge as "a truck has no driver".

## The three best moments

1. **The money fountain.** At "Day in 6 s" cash doubled in eighteen seconds. Loads paid 9 to 18k each, and I climbed to
   #131 of 521 with a "net worth" of 340k. It's thrilling and it costs no effort.
2. **Iuliia, short.** "There it goes. I still like watching them leave." HIDE shrinking her to a chip is exactly right
   for me. And she spoke up at the one moment I needed: "With no lessor, you invoice the shipper, who pays in 30 days."
3. **The few clear cards.** The purchase-loan card (price, tax, weekly payment, "Miss 2 payments and the lender takes
   the truck"), "Cash is low: 772 FD against about 415 FD a week", the dispatcher's "bids keep losing" explanation, and
   "Starting the world…" on the title screen. Plus the art: the snowy dealer lot, Nashville in snow, Dallas lit up green.

## What I did not understand

- What an APU, aero kit, premium tires and sleeper upgrade do, and whether mine paid off.
- What "past its last rebuild" means for the truck I bought, and how long I have before scrap.
- Why a "used lot" truck takes 6 to 10 weeks, and why the wait for new trucks went from 30 to 61 days in two weeks.
- What the lessor's deposit is, what loan-to-value means, and why every BORROW figure is one it won't lend.
- Why every delivery says "Paid Feb 5" on Quick pay, and why "kept 114,733" isn't my cash.
- Why my own truck kept getting 2 to 5/mi loads while the hired drivers often got 8 to 15/mi.
- Whether the two trucks on order will fit, since the yard holds 5 and I'll have 5.
- Why the red badge means "a truck has no driver" one minute and "Rates is new" the next.

## Would I come back tomorrow?

Yes, for the number. Tomorrow I'd set it to Day in 6 s and watch cash climb, which is the most fun I had. But I'd be
coming back to a screensaver. Everything I could do with my money this month was a trap or a locked door, and the
loads ran without me. By the second day I'd want either a reason to touch something or a way to spend 60k on growth.
If the trucks I ordered show up and the yard opens, I'd stay.

## What the game is about, as I understood it

You own trucks, hire people to drive them, and a dispatcher books freight across the country. Money comes in a month
later, unless you pay to get it sooner, and goes out every day on fuel, wages, insurance, repairs and the odd ticket.
You grow by buying more trucks, on cash or on loans, until the yard is full, then you buy a yard. The market swings
from 2 to 15 a mile, and the dispatcher catches some of each. As a skimmer, what I learned is that the fleet makes money
by itself, and the only decisions in front of me were the expensive ones, with nobody slowing me down before I made
them.
