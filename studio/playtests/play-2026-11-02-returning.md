# Play diary: the cautious owner-operator comes back

- **Who I was:** the same careful owner-operator as sitting 1, on the same phone, opening LANES the next evening after a
  day away. I read what Iuliia says, I want to know what a number means before I act, and I'd rather have a sure thing
  than a gamble.
- **Build:** lanes-play `4c931a9`, the build sitting 1's world was made on.
  - The shared clone had since been pulled to `3ccaee9`, so I didn't touch it. Instead I exported `4c931a9` into a
    folder of its own and pointed the port-4180 launch entry at it. Same port, so it's the same browser storage and the
    same saved world. I checked that the page 4180 serves matches that build and differs from 4183's.
  - Sitting 1's own tab was still open on 4180, showing "Fri 00:00 · 15,464 FD · PAUSED". I closed it and came back
    in a fresh tab, the way a player would.
- **Screen and speed:** phone, 375 × 812. Day a minute, plus one pause.
- **How long:** about 5½ real minutes, Fri Feb 2 00:00 to Sun Feb 4 19:00, Year 1. Times below are minutes:seconds from
  the title screen.
- **What I'd left:** Steady Buckeye Haul, Columbus. One leased truck, idle in Jacksonville, dispatcher on. 15,464 FD.
  Paused on Fri Feb 2. The month card had said "earned 49,271, spent 23,798, kept 25,473".
- **Where I ended:** home at Columbus for the weekend, paused Sunday evening, 14,006 FD. Nothing booked yet.
- **Screenshots:** in [play-2026-11-02-returning/](play-2026-11-02-returning/).

## The diary

**0:00 · title.** It's night on the desert road now: stars, the diner, a cactus. There's a big green button, **"CONTINUE ·
STEADY BUCKEYE HAUL, DAY 33"**, above NEW TEST WORLD ([01](play-2026-11-02-returning/01-title-continue-day-33.jpg)).
That's the best thing a returning player can see: my company, by name, waiting. "Day 33" is new to me, though. Last
night the game only ever said weekdays, and I left on "Fri".

**0:08–0:31 · coming back.** I tapped Continue, and for about three seconds nothing happened (the same dead pause New
test world had in sitting 1). Then a loading screen: "Loading your test world · Replaying its history in this
browser." A truck bar ran 24%, 71%, 100% in about fourteen seconds
([02](play-2026-11-02-returning/02-replaying-its-history.jpg)). "Replaying its history" made me nervous. Is it
re-running my month? Could it come out different?

**0:31 · Fri 00:00 · the first screen.** "Fri 00:00 · 15,464 FD · PAUSED". The map is centered on my truck in
Jacksonville, and the sheet reads "Truck 1 · Idle at Jacksonville · round 06:00"
([03](play-2026-11-02-returning/03-first-screen-back.jpg)). Everything matches what I left: the cash, the day, the
truck, "10 d out". My dispatcher is still ticked.

Iuliia's first line is **"Weigh stations pull trucks in; inspections stay on your record two years. Tired drivers break
rules; home time is cheaper than fines."** That's the tutorial lesson I'd stopped on, not anything about tonight. There's
no "welcome back", no "last time you…", and no mention of the month I'd just finished. The yard card says "Now 0
tractors", which, read cold, looks like I have no truck.

**0:44 · What now?** The obvious button for someone coming back. It said **"Step 8 of 13 · Find your safety rating on
Company"** ([04](play-2026-11-02-returning/04-what-now-step-8.jpg)). My truck is 894 miles from home and idle, and the
game's answer to "what now" is a tutorial chore. Its buttons sit below the fold, but this time scrolling the sheet got
me past the card, so I wasn't trapped the way I was in sitting 1.

**1:00 · Money.** The page was unchanged: 15,464, "Cushion: 1.7 weeks", and "Owed to you · Nothing owed yet. Your first
invoice lands here." after twelve paid loads. The month card had told me to "Read the month on Money", but there's no
month on it.

**1:35 · Fri 00:00 · the board, and a real decision.** "Loads from Jacksonville · 2 · next round 06:00". The only two I
could book were Sanford → Des Moines and Tampa → Rochester, both 2,040 FD at about 1.45 a mile, marked **"−1,142"** and
**"−1,283 FD at book-now"** ([05](play-2026-11-02-returning/05-two-loads-both-lose-money.jpg)). Everything toward home
(Charleston → North Canton, Summerville → Lima) said "Local carriers first this round". Last week I was getting 3 to 5 a
mile.

So the second evening has a decision the first didn't. I'm stuck in a dead market 894 miles from home on a Friday:
eat a loss, run home empty, or wait a round. The first evening was only ever "which good load".

**1:43–2:16 · Fri 06:00–13:00 · waiting a round.** One tap on the speed button gave Live pace, two gave Day a minute.
The 06:00 round came and went with nothing booked. At 13:00 a red toast finally gave the real reason: **"Truck 1 has been
idle 1 d at Jacksonville: Every load in range requires vetting you don't pass: authority under 90 days old."**
([06](play-2026-11-02-returning/06-idle-the-real-reason.jpg)). The board had said "local carriers first". Two
different walls, and only one of them was true for me. My dispatcher couldn't have booked anything here all weekend, and
nothing told me that when I arrived.

**2:26 · Fri 17:00 · going home.** I pressed SEND HOME · 894 MI and got "Heading home for days off"
([07](play-2026-11-02-returning/07-heading-home-for-days-off.jpg)). No price before or after; I didn't know what 894
empty miles would cost me.

**2:50–3:26 · Saturday · the run.** 503 miles from Fri 17:00 to Sat 02:00 without a stop. Then only 34 miles in the
next ten hours, a rest near Knoxville that nothing announced
([08](play-2026-11-02-returning/08-rested-near-knoxville.jpg)). To pause I had to tap through Day in 6 s and Day in 2 s,
and five game hours slipped by.

**4:20 · Sun 02:00 · home.** "Truck 1 · Home time at Columbus". Cash 15,431 → **14,006**: the run home cost about
1,425 FD, which wasn't shown anywhere ([09](play-2026-11-02-returning/09-home-time-cash-down-1425.jpg)). The losing load
I'd turned down would have cost 1,142. Going home was the cautious choice, and it was the more expensive one, and I
couldn't have known.

**4:30 · the month is gone.** Money's chart now reads **"CASH, LAST 3 DAYS"** and starts at my return
([10](play-2026-11-02-returning/10-cash-last-3-days.jpg)). My whole first month's line is gone.

**5:20 · Sun 19:00 · the home board.** Paused. "Loads from Columbus · 297 · next round 06:00". The nearest was Columbus →
Sterling Heights, 229 mi, 2,781 FD at **12.14/mi, "+2,248 at book-now"**
([11](play-2026-11-02-returning/11-home-board-12-a-mile.jpg)). Florida paid 1.44 at a loss and home pays twelve.
Monday's choice will be the same as every choice last night: book the top of the board. I stopped there. Iuliia's line
never changed the whole sitting.

## The five things that most got in the way

Ranked by how a returning player would feel them.

1. **Nothing tells me where I left off.**
   - There's no recap of the last delivery, the month result, how far the truck is from home, or why it's idle.
   - Iuliia's first line is a stale tutorial lesson about weigh stations, and it stays all sitting.
   - "What now?" answers with tutorial step 8, not with my truck.
   - The title's Continue button is the only thing that knows I'm back.
2. **The real reason I was stuck came a day late and contradicted the board.**
   - The board said "Local carriers first this round". After a day idle, a toast said "authority under 90 days old".
   - The dispatcher I'd left on could never have booked anything in that market, and nothing said so when I opened the
     game.
3. **The decision I finally got wasn't informed.** SEND HOME shows no cost. The 894 empty miles took about 1,425 FD,
   more than the 1,142 the losing load would have. A cautious player picks the safe-looking option, and the game
   punished it without saying so.
4. **The month I came back to has vanished.**
   - Money has no month report, though the month card said to read it there.
   - The cash chart says "last 3 days".
   - Owed-to-you still says "Your first invoice lands here" after twelve loads.
   - The title's "Day 33" doesn't match any date the game shows (it only shows "Fri").
5. **Coming back is slow and odd on a phone.**
   - Continue does nothing for three seconds, then "Replaying its history in this browser" runs for about fourteen.
   - Pausing means tapping through the two fastest speeds.
   - There's no news ticker at phone size, so everything that happened arrives as one toast at a time.

## The three best moments

1. **The Continue button with my company's name on it.** I knew at once I was coming back to my world, and nothing
   was lost: cash, day, truck and my dispatcher setting were exactly as I'd left them.
2. **The Florida trap.** It's a real second-evening decision: not "which good load" but "how do I get out of a dead
   market on a Friday". That's trucking, and the first evening didn't have it.
3. **Coming home to Columbus.** 297 loads and twelve a mile at the top. Home felt like home.

## What I did not understand

- What "Day 33" counts from.
- Why the board said "local carriers first" when the problem was my authority's age, and when 90 days comes.
- What the 1,425 FD going home paid for.
- Whether the dispatcher would ever have taken one of the losing loads if I'd left it.
- Where the first month's report lives now.
- What "Replaying its history in this browser" means for me: is my world being re-run, and could it come out
  different?

## Would I come back tomorrow?

Yes. Everything was where I left it, I'm home, and Monday's board pays well. But the second evening started with a lesson
about weigh stations while my truck sat stranded 894 miles away, and I only learned why a day later. If the game
greeted me with "You're 894 mi from home in a dead market. New carriers can't book here until day 90. Going home costs
about 1,400; this load loses 1,142", I'd have made the same choice in ten seconds and felt clever doing it. Coming back
should feel like walking into my own office, not like reopening a manual.

## What the game is about, as I understood it

You keep one leased truck earning in a live freight market, and on this test page the world waits while you're away.
Coming back is about where your truck is and what the market around it pays: a good market is one tap, a dead one is a
real decision between a losing load, a long empty run home or a day's wait. The first month taught me the board; the
second evening taught me geography. The game knows everything I needed to decide well, but tonight it told me only after
I'd already chosen.
