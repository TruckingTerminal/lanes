# Play diary: a cautious owner-operator's first month

- **Who I was:** a careful owner-operator on a phone who has never seen LANES. I read everything Iuliia says, I want to
  know what a number means before I act on it, and I'd rather have a sure load than a gamble.
- **Build:** lanes-play `4c931a9` ("Deploy 6389e77 from lanes", 2026-11-02 23:27 UTC), my own copy on port 4180, a fresh
  browser profile.
- **Screen and speed:** phone, 375 × 812. Mostly "Day a minute". I sped up through home time and paused at the moments
  I wanted to choose a load.
- **How long:** 40 real minutes, Wed Jan 3 to Fri Feb 2, Year 1. Minutes below are real minutes from opening the page.
- **Where I ended:** Steady Buckeye Haul, Columbus. One leased truck, 12 loads delivered, 83% on time, 15,464 FD in cash
  (I started with 4,700), #317 of 521 carriers. The truck is idle in Jacksonville with the dispatcher on. Paused.
- **Screenshots:** in [play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/).

## The diary

**Min 0 · title.** A rig rolls toward me down a desert road at dusk. It's lovely, and a diner slides past now and then.
There's one button, NEW TEST WORLD, plus a "≡" in the corner that I took for a menu. It's actually full screen, and
tapping it did nothing I could see. I pressed New test world, and for about three seconds nothing happened. I nearly
pressed it again.

**Min 1 · Jan 3, 06:00 · the map.** Iuliia's first line: "This is America's freight market, live: every truck you see is
real and moving. First, pick a home city." That's a good opening, and I believed her. The zoom box in the corner reads
"− FIT + FIT", with two FITs ([01](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/01-zoom-control-fit-fit.png)).

**Min 2 · picking home.** Three starter cities, each with one honest line. Columbus: "the insurance is the gentlest on
the list." I'm cautious, so I picked Columbus. On the Columbus page Iuliia's picture turned into an empty black square
([02](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/02-blank-portrait-on-columbus.jpg)). Her line there was "Read the costs
before the character." I still don't know what "the character" means. The two buttons float over the text, and the
text keeps going underneath them into a black band ([03](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/03-hub-sheet-text-under-buttons.jpg)).
The money is called FD, and nothing tells me what FD is.

**Min 3 · the board, before committing.** I tapped SEE THE BOARD first, the way you'd look at the lot before signing.
There were 24 loads, each showing a posted rate and "usually clears near" a lower one. As a trucker I read that as
freight getting bid down, which made sense. Nothing said which trailer each load needs, even though the hub page had
just told me "Van strong · Reefer even". The town names had no states. "Newark → Philadelphia · 490 mi" made me
think the miles were broken until I worked out it was Newark, Ohio. Same with "Delaware → Laredo" (Delaware, Ohio).

**Min 4 · naming the company.** The name box came pre-filled with "Scioto Freight Lines". That's a real local touch and I
liked it. I renamed it Steady Buckeye Haul. "Owner-operator: The live world's start" doesn't tell me what I get (money?
a truck?). I found out on the next screen.

**Min 5 · Jan 3 · first real screen.** It opened already scrolled down, so the first thing I saw was a pretty snowy yard
picture and my truck card, with no Iuliia ([04](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/04-first-screen-no-iuliia.jpg)).
I found her by scrolling up: "One leased Workhorse, a dry van, and you driving. The lessor keeps 15% of every load."
I had 4,700 FD. WHAT NOW? said "Bid on Delaware to Laredo… pays about 3,971 FD over break-even." Its two buttons were cut
down to a 4-pixel teal sliver above the tab bar ([05](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/05-what-now-buttons-cut-off.jpg)).
1,479 miles to the Mexican border on day one is not what I'd call cautious. But she's the guide, and it pays nearly my
whole bank balance again.

**Min 5–6 · the load page, then stuck.** The load page is good: deliver-by date, driving hours, "Reload at Laredo: good",
book-now 4.76/mi, my break-even 2.08, profit +3,971, ceiling 4.89. The bid stepper started at 4.81, *above* book-now,
which I couldn't make sense of. I tapped the break-even number for her explanation. Fuel .86 + driver .92 + upkeep .28
+ tolls .02 already makes 2.08, and then a line "÷ 85% for the lessor… included" leaves it at 2.08. So where did the
lessor go? The profit card says "break-even times all the miles you'd drive" and then uses 1,479, not 1,479 + 28 empty.
Then I couldn't get out of the card. CLOSE and WHAT NOW sit under the tab bar. Tapping outside, swiping it down and
tapping the handle did nothing, and switching tabs carried the card along with me
([06](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/06-stuck-in-break-even-card.jpg)). On a phone my only way out was
reloading the page. That's the moment a stranger decides the game is broken.

**Min 6 · reload.** It remembered me ("CONTINUE · STEADY BUCKEYE HAUL, DAY 3") and came back paused: "Welcome back. The
clock is paused; press a speed or 1–4 to run it." There are no 1–4 keys on a phone, but I was relieved. The Loads tab
shows states ("Long Beach, NY", mystery solved) and a "+X FD at book-now" on every row, which is the one number I want.
Some loads are greyed out with "Needs 30-day authority". That's real trucking, and I liked it.

**Min 6 · Jan 3 · booked Laredo.** I took book-now, because a sure thing is the cautious move. Iuliia: "From now on your
dispatcher bids when you don't. Check the rules before you go." I opened the rules: bid at break-even +25%, empty up to
150 mi, home after 21 days, and more under "More rules". None of them has an explanation. The labels don't open
anything, and Iuliia doesn't say how "bid at +25%" and "never below +5%" fit together. I set +60%, never below +25%,
between rounds +50%, home after 10 days, and saved. (Help explained it all later, under "Standing orders". Help calls the
Truck tab "the Fleet tab".)

**Min 7 · Jan 3–5 · driving.** Getting to "Day a minute" took two taps on the one speed button, which is fine. Watching
the truck drift down past Louisville, Nashville and Memphis, with the map's city art passing under it, was a joy.
"Tonight, as planned: a rest area near Brownsville": I read Texas and thought it was wrong, but it was Brownsville,
Tennessee. My cash dropped 32 FD a day and nothing told me why. The Loads tab while driving says "loads show when it's
free". I wanted to line up my reload before I got to Laredo, the way any dispatcher would.

**Min 8 · Help.** I read "Your first week" because I'm the type. It's clear and plain. It says I start with 5,000 FD; I
started with 4,700. A used truck costs about a quarter of 182,000, and my lease deposit counts toward it.

**Min 9 · Jan 5, 16:00 · first payday.** "Delivered Delaware to Laredo · On time." The settlement card is great as a
picture: load 7,046, fuel, tolls, my pay for driving, upkeep, the lessor's 15%, then **"Held toward your own truck
−2,114"**, and at the bottom "Yours 1,273 FD"
([07](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/07-first-payday.jpg), [08](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/08-first-payday-the-hold.jpg)).
Iuliia had said +3,971. Nobody had told me 30% would be held back. The hold is the only line on the card you can't tap
for an explanation, and the log line only says "after fuel, tolls and the lessor's cut". I tapped "Your pay for
driving" and got "What you pay yourself for driving". If I'm paying myself, why did my cash only go up by 1,273? Then I
got stuck in that card too, and reloaded again ([09](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/09-stuck-again-while-eta-goes-late.jpg)).
The dispatcher had already "Won Laredo to Fort Wayne, 1,443 mi at 4.74/mi" before the 18:00 round. It's a good load,
nearly home, but I never got a turn.

**Min 10 · Jan 6 · Rates opens.** The badge took me to More, where Rates was new. "Theft risk is against a quiet road",
and a THEFT column. The table is wider than my phone, so two columns are cut off
([10](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/10-rates-table-cut-off.jpg)). 45 of 60 markets read exactly "2.50 Hot".
The best lane on the board, Boise → Denver at 20.98/mi, would make any trucker laugh.

**Min 10 · More › Iuliia.** By accident I found her whole walkthrough: "4 of 13: Getting paid". This is where she explains
the hold: "The lease also holds a little back from each load toward a truck of your own… so don't mourn it." 30% isn't a
little. Her writing is the best thing in the game ("Companies that live on quick pay receive a goodbye note from me.
Handwritten. Fountain pen."). But it lives in a drawer, and on payday it wasn't where I was. She says "On Money, find the
lessor's share and the hold." I followed GO TO MONEY, and Money shows neither. Money did have "The token", though:
"FD/USDC pool", "your FD above working capital is burned", "airdrop", and a button, BID IN THE OPENING AUCTION, in
dollars. I came to haul freight. A real-money button under my weekly costs is where a cautious person starts to wonder
what this is. I didn't touch it.

**Min 11 · the speed button.** I wanted to slow down. The one button only goes forward: Paused → Live → Day a minute →
Day in 6 s → Day in 2 s → Paused. A tap registered late and I overshot. Getting back to Paused meant riding through
"Day in 2 s", and three game days went by in ten seconds. I missed a delivery and a booking.

**Min 13 · Jan 9 · "Late 16 h".** Fort Wayne was delivered 16 hours late. The badge wraps onto two lines ("Late 16 / h"),
tapping it does nothing, and nobody says why
([11](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/11-late-16h-badge-wraps.jpg)). The last time I'd looked it was "On time".
The dispatcher had already sent me 993 more miles to Graham, Texas, even though I'm 6 days out and home was 170 miles
away. WHAT NOW? said "Step 5 of 13 · Pick the next load from Find loads", but the load was already picked, and there is
no "Find loads".

**Min 14 · Jan 10–11 · the ETA.** On the way to Graham the ETA badge went On time → Late 17 h → Late 5 h → Late 10 h →
Late 14 h → Late 9 h ([12](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/12-eta-late-17h-at-night.jpg)). It slides an hour
later for every hour the driver sleeps, so every night it goes red. After a day I stopped believing it. There was one
nice line on the way: "clean level 1 inspection at the Marshall scale (I-70)." Graham was delivered late 10 h. With
the round one game hour away (2.5 real seconds), I tried to pause, rode through the fast speeds again, and the
dispatcher booked Dallas → Prairieville at 3.77/mi, half the last rate.

**Min 16 · Jan 11 · I take the wheel back.** I turned the dispatcher off. The checkbox saves by itself and quietly put
my numbers back to the defaults (60% → 25%, 25% → 5%) ([13](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/13-dispatcher-off-resets-rules.jpg)).
Prairieville was delivered on time and paid me **−32 FD** after the hold. The robot had booked me a load that cost
money. Iuliia's caption still said "your dispatcher bids when you don't".

**Min 18 · Jan 12 · my first real choice since day one.** I paused on time and read the Prairieville board. I wanted
home, so I booked Lake Charles → Lexington, KY (962 mi, +1,771, deliver by Jan 18). It felt good. Then the truck sat
"Loading at Lake Charles" for 33 game hours, all weekend, without a word
([15](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/15-loading-all-weekend.jpg)). Maybe the shipper was closed for the weekend.
Nobody said.

**Min 19 · Company and Intel.** "Loads delivered 4 · 50% on time": both late ones were the dispatcher's, and they're on
my record. There's a policy page where I could choose to run 15 hours a day and 56 mph "always". I stayed legal.
Insurance says 89 FD a week here and 0 FD on Money. There's a NEW TEST WORLD button at the very bottom of my Company
page, right above the tab bar. Intel lists 581 companies, and nearly all of them are "Fleet-18", "Fleet-44"… The
rivals feel like bots.

**Min 21 · Jan 15 · cushion scare.** On Money, "Cushion: 0.9 weeks of your own spending" with 8,922 FD. The explainer
says I average 9,392 FD a week in "spending", which must count the lessor, fuel and the hold. It reads like I'm a week
from broke. Then I was stuck in that card as well ([14](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/14-cushion-card-stuck.jpg)),
and that was reload number three.

**Min 23 · Jan 16 · Lexington, then home.** Lexington was on time: 4,064 FD and **331** in my pocket. There was a "SEND
HOME · 202 MI" button, but before I could press it the truck was already "Running empty to Columbus". My 10-day rule had
fired even with the dispatcher off. It was the right call, but nothing logged it. "You're home for 5 days off." That's a
nice beat, and then it's five game days of staring at a countdown, so I sped through it. Home time ended about a day
early. A card said "Truck 1 has been idle 1 d 13 h… Your dispatcher has no bid order… sat out 11 bidding
rounds" (11 rounds in a day and a half?), with RESTORE THE DEFAULT DISPATCHER / BID BY HAND. It was clear and useful,
and I'd missed it.

**Min 26–31 · Jan 22–26 · the good stretch.** The Columbus board had 303 loads, and rates had tripled while I was home:
Cleveland at 14.28/mi, Lexington at 12.78. Nobody said "boom". Book-now was the ceiling, two to three times what
loads "usually clear near", so I just booked. I ran Columbus → Cleveland → Meads, KY → Newark, NJ → New York City → Columbus,
**all on time, all chosen by me**, and cash went from 8,700 to 15,670. This was the game I wanted. A few things still
jarred. "Tonight, as planned: the shoulder. Nowhere legal to stop in reach." That's my careful driver sleeping on the
shoulder outside New York "as planned", with no say. The 18:00 round fired while my thumb was on NYC → Springfield, the
card vanished, and the board reshuffled. Zero tolls into New Jersey. The truck's condition sat at 85% for 7,000 miles.

**Min 33 · Jan 27 · putting the phone down.** I booked Columbus → Indianapolis, on time, +892. Then I did what I'd do
before bed and turned the dispatcher back on. It showed the defaults again, not my numbers, so I set 60% and 100 mi.
It sat a day ("49 dry van loads in range pay enough, but the dispatcher's bids keep losing", an honest and good
message), then won Indianapolis → Philadelphia at **3.01/mi** (paid me −139) and Philadelphia → Fort Lauderdale at
3.87/mi. Florida is the loosest market on the Rates page. Help says "you can always beat the best of them by hand."
Boy, can you.

**Min 35 · Jan 29 · Business.** Yards, shops, fuel stations and truck stops, 130k to 770k FD each. With 16k, every FD
button is greyed. The only lit button on the page is **"SMALL YARD · $900"**, then "$2,615 / $6,538 / $11,569 to the
launch pool", and "half of every price is burned" ([16](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/16-business-only-lit-button-dollars.jpg)).
That page is where I decided the game wants my card. I didn't tap anything.

**Min 37 · Feb 1–2 · month close.** Economy opened ([17](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/17-economy-page.jpg)):
a public ledger "the way EVE Online's economists publish theirs", "650 shoulder tickets", "Cargo stolen 28 loads". It's
interesting to a nerd and alien to a trucker. Then Iuliia: **"Your first month: earned 49,271, spent 23,798, kept 25,473
FD."** That's a lovely line ([18](play-2026-11-02-cautious-owner-operator/18-first-month-summary.jpg)). But my cash says
15,464. Is the other 10k the hold? It's never shown anywhere. "Read the month on Money": Money is the same page as
always, with no month on it. I paused and put the phone down.

## The five things that most got in the way

Ranked by how a stranger would feel them.

1. **Iuliia's explanation cards trap you.** On a phone, CLOSE and WHAT NOW sit under the tab bar. Tapping outside,
   swiping, tapping the handle, Escape and changing tabs all fail, and the card follows you between tabs. I reloaded the
   page three times to escape. The game teaches you to tap numbers, and the tap becomes a dead end. A stranger thinks it
   froze and leaves.
2. **The money never matches what I was told.** "+3,971 profit" became 1,273 in hand. 30% is "held toward your own
   truck", and no screen shows how much is held. Iuliia sends me to Money to find the hold and it isn't there. "Your pay
   for driving" is charged to me, the driver. "Cushion 0.9 weeks" when I'm fine. "Kept 25,473" with 15,464 in cash.
   "Next truck 5,366 FD away" turned into 45,799. It feels like being shorted, not taught.
3. **I barely got to choose.** After load one the dispatcher is on by default, and at Day a minute it books before I
   can look, sometimes before the round. Its picks were my only late deliveries and my two money-losers, and it sent me
   to Texas twice and Florida once. The one speed button can only reach Paused by going through the fastest speeds. The
   board reshuffles under my thumb. Loads are hidden while I drive. Turning the dispatcher off or on wipes my rules.
4. **Iuliia isn't where I am.** Her first caption was scrolled off the top of my first real screen. Her line stuck on
   "Check the rules before you go" for four weeks, even after I'd turned the dispatcher off. WHAT NOW? told me to pick a
   load that was already picked. The 13-step walkthrough that explains everything is in a drawer under More, and I found
   it by accident. She said nothing at my first late delivery, my first negative payday, or the weekend at the dock.
5. **Numbers I couldn't trust.** The ETA goes red every night and swings by 12 hours. Two deliveries were late with no
   reason. Rates tripled with no news. In the boom, book-now equals the ceiling at two to three times the clearing price. The bid stepper
   starts above book-now. Insurance is 0 here and 89 a week there. Condition stayed at 85% forever. Most markets read
   "2.50 Hot". Each one is small, but together they made me stop reading the numbers, and the numbers are the game.

## The three best moments

1. **The week I ran it myself (Jan 22–26).** Cleveland → Meads → Newark → New York → Columbus, all on time, all my picks,
   cash nearly doubling, home at the end. That's the game, and it's good.
2. **The map and the road.** My truck crossing the country over hand-drawn cities (the San Antonio tower, the Houston
   stadium, the New Orleans dome, rain over Jacksonville), the title screen's rig at dusk, and "You're home for 5 days
   off."
3. **Iuliia's voice.** "Handwritten. Fountain pen." "The way my grandmother watched the samovar." "Tolstoy had his
   estate; I have charts." And the month close: "Your first month: earned 49,271, spent 23,798, kept 25,473 FD." When
   she's in the right place, she's the reason to keep going. (Runner-up: the idle card that told me exactly why the
   dispatcher kept losing.)

## What I did not understand

- What FD is. Money? The token? Why it gets "burned", what the "opening auction" in dollars is, and what my "airdrop" of
  Miles means.
- The hold: how much I've got, where it lives, why it was 30% every time and 25% once, and whether "kept 25,473" includes
  it.
- Why "Your pay for driving" is a cost when I am the driver, and where that money went.
- Why anyone bids when book-now is the ceiling, and why the bid stepper starts above book-now.
- Why my dispatcher wins loads at 3/mi while I book the same market at 10–14/mi.
- Why Fort Wayne and Graham were late, and why Lake Charles took 33 hours to load.
- The numbers on the map labels ("COLUMBUS36"), "Read the costs before the character", "Vetting open", "On time —",
  "+5.3 bid-score points", the "Cass dial".
- Why the next-truck goal jumped from 5,366 to 45,799, and why "Your company so far" lists none of my firsts.
- Insurance 0 FD on Money versus 4,642 a year on Company.

## Would I come back tomorrow?

Probably, once. The last ten days were good enough that I want to see whether I can own my truck, and I want to know
where my held money is. But I'd come back wary, for two reasons. If an explanation card traps me again in the first
five minutes, I'm done, because a game that punishes me for asking what a number means isn't one I can learn. And the
real-dollar buttons, the "burned" FD and the auction make a cautious trucker wonder what he's really signing up for.
If Iuliia were standing next to me at payday and the cards let me go, I'd come back every night.

## What the game is about, as I understood it

You run a one-truck trucking company in a live national freight market. Every load is a lane with a rate. You book it
or bid on it, then live with the miles, the empty run to the next pickup, the weekend dock, the night on the shoulder,
and the reload where you land. You start leased: the lessor takes 15% and the lease holds back a share of every load
toward a truck of your own, and getting that truck is the first real goal. Past that come drivers, dispatchers, yards,
shops and truck stops. Your standing is counted in Miles that someday turn into a token. Underneath it all is a market
that booms and busts on its own. What I learned in the first month is that the market rewards the player who picks
loads by hand and punishes the one who leaves it to the dispatcher.
