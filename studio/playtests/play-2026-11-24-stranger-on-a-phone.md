# Play test 2026-11-24: the stranger on a phone

Build 2ce9b53, server lanes-player-1 (localhost:4181), phone size 375 x 812. A friend sent me a link and I have twenty
minutes on the couch. I've never driven a truck or run a business. I tap what looks tappable and do what Julia says.
I played from the title screen to game day 36 (Thu Feb 15, Year 1). About 105 tool calls, 19 screenshots.

## 1. The verdict

- **Would I come back tomorrow?** Probably not. It's beautiful, and the first load felt great. But after that a robot
  plays for me, the money barely moves, and Julia says my first real goal is "around four months" away.
- I'd only come back if my friend asked "how's your company doing?" Then I'd open it to see what the robot did.
- **What I think the game is about:** you start with one rented truck, pick freight off a board, and save up the cut
  you're forced to keep until you can buy your own truck, then more trucks.

## 3. The diary

**Minute 0 · title screen.** Night desert, a diner glowing, a big rig with its headlights on. Wow. One button: "NEW
TEST WORLD". Test? Am I testing something? I tap it anyway.

**Minute 1 · Jan 11 06:00 (day 1).** A map of America with hundreds of tiny squares, and Julia: a blonde woman with a
clipboard. Her first joke about birthdays made me smile. My first tap on NEXT did nothing; the second one worked.

**Minute 2 · choosing a home.** Los Angeles, Columbus or Chicago. Columbus says "the insurance is the gentlest on the
list", which sounds like easy mode, so Columbus. The next page is a fact sheet: "13 loads posted last round · 5.01/mi
average", "2.0 loads per truck · hot", "Reload: fair", "Yard: 22 FD a week". I don't know what FD is. I tap START HERE.

**Minute 3 · naming.** The name box already says "Scioto Freight Lines". When I typed, my name didn't replace it; I
had to clear the box first. Now I'm Couch Potato Freight. I pick the woman driver portrait. "Owner-operator. The live
world's start." or "Small fleet ... to test the fleet game." I take the first one because it's on top.

**Minute 4 · my truck.** Julia, 3 short pages: one leased truck, the lessor keeps 15%, a waiting truck costs money.
Fine. On the phone her card is scrolled, so the top line of her message is cut off ("says one leased Workhorse...").
Then "SHOW ME THE BOARD".

**Minute 5 · the board.** Julia, 5 more pages. "The chip on the right is the number that matters." On my phone the
chip is under the card, not on the right, and there are two numbers in it: "+1,285 · cash 877". Which one is the
chip? The NEXT button moves up and down between pages, so I keep missing it. 28 loads, each with four lines of
numbers. Julia's tip: green chip, few empty miles. Gahanna to Bridgeport: 9 miles empty, +1,285. That one.

**Minute 6 · the load page.** Route, deadlines, "a 736 FD claim and a mark on your record" if I'm late, break-even,
ceiling, vetting. Too much. But it says "Book now is the sure price." So BOOK NOW. The clock jumped to "DAY A MINUTE"
on its own and the truck started moving on the map. That felt great. **Best moment.**

**Minute 7 · Thu 13:00.** Julia: "Set your dispatcher before you go." I pressed NEXT expecting her to take me there.
She didn't; she said "Money and More are open now". A bit later: "Turn your dispatcher on (its card is on the Truck
tab)". I scrolled way down and found a box, "Bid for me while I'm away". I ticked it. "Dispatcher saved." OK.

**Minute 8 · Thu 21:00.** Julia gave me a long lesson, "The truck rolls", about 11-hour days, fuel and sleeping, which
arrived after the truck had already been rolling for hours. The fleet card had said "Tonight, as planned: a rest area
near Hopatcong Hills". Now it says "No legal stop is in reach tonight, so it sleeps on the shoulder: a 250 FD ticket if
a patrol comes by." Why did the plan change? I'm 98 miles from the end. Nobody told me.

**Minute 9 · Fri (day 2).** Delivered! A receipt card: load 2,943, fuel −497, lessor −441... "Held toward your own
truck (kept) −883", "Yours 937 FD". The board said +1,285. Where's the rest? I think the 883 is savings they force
me to keep. Then: "Won Newark to Sunbury, your dispatcher's bid." My robot found me a job on its own. Magic.
**Second best moment.**

**Minute 10 · the speed button.** I wanted it faster. I tapped the speed button; nothing seemed to happen, so I tapped
again; later it said "DAY IN 2 S", then "every real minute", and finally "DAY IN 6 S". It took me about five taps to
land where I wanted. The taps seemed to happen late.

**Minute 11 · Mon Jan 15 (day 5).** Julia: "What it costs to exist." If I'm 2,000 under for four weekly checks in a
row, the company is wound up. Scary but clear. She says "The Money tab lists twenty days of it." Money shows cash,
"Lease minimum 318 FD", "749 Miles · your standing in the beta", "The Reserve", "The token (later)". Token? Is this
crypto? I don't see twenty days of anything.

**Minute 12 · More tab.** A list of things that will open later. The Dealer says: buy once you have 80,300 FD; "8% of
the way to a sound truck". Finally a goal I understand. Also "Logbook: opens at the token's launch, Jan 1, Year 3".
Year 3?

**Minute 14 · Sat Jan 20 (day 10).** "Truck 1 is idle at Clearwater: nothing in reach pays." I'm in Florida! It
gave me four "Ways out" buttons (Orlando, Jacksonville, Savannah, Atlanta). I was choosing when the screen changed and
the buttons were gone. My dispatcher had won "Brandon to Fort Wayne, 1,153 mi at 1.86/mi". My first load was 4.72 a
mile.

**Minute 15 · Wed Jan 24 (day 14).** "Delivered · Brandon → Fort Wayne · −17 FD yours." My truck drove 1,153 miles,
two days, and I *lost* 17. Meanwhile Julia's step "7 of 13 · Compare revenue against costs on Money" has sat there for
days; Money has no revenue line to compare. **This is where I'd put the phone down for good.** The game picked a bad
job for me, made me pay for it, and the help told me to look at a page that doesn't have the thing in it.

**Second chance, minute 17 · Sat Jan 27 (day 17).** OK, one more go. Rates are lower now (2.79/mi). Julia: "To a
sound first truck: 67,919 FD." I open More → Rates because it says New. It's a spreadsheet: six columns, cut off on
the right on my phone. Every city says "Hot", even ones with 1.7 loads per truck. West coast says 6.00, everything
there is "Hot" too.

**Minute 19 · Wed Jan 31 (day 21).** I open the dispatcher's "More rules". In the middle of a paragraph: "the eased
floor can go as far as 12% under break-even." So that's why. My robot is allowed to lose money. I tick "Move idle
trucks to busier hubs" because Florida. Every tick box looks the same, and the % sign drops onto its own line under
the box.

**Minute 21 · Wed Feb 7 (day 28).** A big one: McAllen to Florissant, 6,277 FD, 2,181 mine. Feels good. But Julia's
"To a sound first truck" went from 67,919 (day 17) to 67,730 (day 28). Eleven days of driving and the goal moved 189?

**Minute 22 · Mon Feb 12 (day 33).** "Past months" appeared on Money. January: earned 21,887, spent on fuel,
lessor, tolls... kept 11,109. 12 loads, 100% on time, 83% loaded miles. This is the page I wanted on day 5. Then a
pop-up with a stamp: "Columbus docks wave you ahead · tier 3 of 10." I got promoted by the docks! **Third best
moment.** One odd line: the payout cards said my driver's draw "stays with you"; this page says it "stayed with the
company". Which is it?

**Minute 24 · Thu Feb 15 (day 36).** I tried Help. It didn't open on the first or second tap; the docks pop-up was
over it. The third time it did: a long, clear table of contents. Help says a used truck is "about a quarter of the
182,000 FD a new rig costs" (so about 45,000). The Dealer said 80,300. Julia now says: "a sound truck is about 59
loads away at your recent average, around four months. You're 17% of the way." Four months. I stop here: cash 13,786
FD, about 11,000 held toward the truck, 10,957 "Miles".

(The browser pane sometimes stopped redrawing; a Shift key press woke it. Screenshots then lagged a step behind what
the text said. That's the test setup, not the game, I think.)

## 5. The three best moments and the five things that most got in the way

Best:
1. Booking the first load: the clock speeds up by itself and the little truck drives off across a snowy map.
2. The first "Won ..., your dispatcher's bid": the company works while I'm on the couch.
3. The January report card with its desk art, and "Columbus docks wave you ahead · tier 3 of 10".

Got in the way:
1. My own dispatcher lost me money on its biggest haul, and the reason was hidden in a paragraph.
2. Numbers everywhere: every load card has about ten figures, and the one Julia calls "the chip" shows two.
3. Julia sends me to look for things that aren't there yet (revenue vs costs on Money).
4. After the first load I'm not playing, I'm watching. The goal is "around four months" off.
5. The phone layout: the cut-off table, the jumping NEXT button, pop-ups over buttons, long scrolls to the dispatcher.

## 6. What I never found or never understood

- What FD is. I guess it's the money. Nobody said.
- What "Miles · your standing in the beta", "The Reserve" and "The token (later)" are, and whether this is a crypto game.
- Who I'm competing with. Help says "every other company is a player or an AI", but I never saw one, never lost a
  bid that I know of, never saw a name.
- Bidding. I only used Book now; the "−  +  range 2.27–5.15  BID" box scared me off.
- "Hot", "tight", "reload: fair", "out/in", "headhaul", "vetting", "ceiling".
- Company, Intel, Standing, Finance, Economy, Business, Brokerage, Back Lot: never opened. Home time: never used; my
  driver (me!) was "11 d out" and I don't know if that hurts.
- Why the robot slept on the shoulder when it had planned a rest area.
- Whether the forced 30% "held toward your truck" is mine to take out ("A withdrawal reaches cash the next day"?).

## 7. Then and now (against play-2026-11-10-cautious-owner-operator)

- **Gone:** the guide that never mentioned the dispatcher. Julia now says to turn it on right after the first load, and it worked at once.
- **Gone:** the loaded-miles bug in the monthly report. January read "5,011 of 6,073 miles loaded (83%)", which is believable.
- **Better:** the cash forecast. The load page said "Cash now 877 FD" and the payout was 937 (in November it was 2,576 vs 2,082). The board's "+1,285" chip still reads as the money you get, and it isn't.
- **Still there:** the stranded-truck "Ways out" card (still good), but now the dispatcher can act before the player taps it.
- **Still there, smaller:** several first-truck numbers at once (Dealer 80,300 cash, Help "a quarter of 182,000", Julia "64,472 to go"). Julia's new line, "about 59 loads away … around four months … 17% of the way", is one clear answer.
- **Still there, in a new form:** a guide step that dead-ends. In November it was step 10, sending a one-truck owner to a locked Staff page. Now it's step 7, "Compare revenue against costs on Money", when Money has nothing to compare until the month closes.
- **Not seen this time:** the 21-day forced deadhead home. The dispatcher was on and my driver reached "11 d out" with no empty run home.
- **New and better:** the clock goes to "a day a minute" by itself after the first booking; the January report card; the "docks wave you ahead · tier 3 of 10" reward; Help's table of contents.
- **New and worse:** with Julia's defaults, the dispatcher took a 1,153-mile haul at 1.86/mi for −17 FD. A stranger meets that before any of the good parts above.
