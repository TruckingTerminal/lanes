LANES lanes run on a real road graph since config 0.10.0 (Jeff asked for all interstates, the US highway system, realistic tolls, and higher weather risk on highways). Source: Natural Earth 1:10m `ne_10m_roads_north_america` (public domain), class Interstate + Federal/US + every Tollway + CA-99. Generated file `packages/data/src/roads.ts` (~500 KB, varint/base64); decoder + Dijkstra/A* in `packages/data/src/roadNetwork.ts`; `lanes.ts` routes every hub pair (miles, hours, tollFd, highwayShare, edge path).

Build tool: `packages/data/tools/build-roads.mjs` + `shp.mjs`; data (not in the repo) in this session's scratchpad `roads-data/na.{shp,dbf}` (downloaded from github nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/10m_cultural). Run: `node packages/data/tools/build-roads.mjs na.shp na.dbf <repo> --tol 0.03 [--stats]`. Lessons: junction grid must be tight (0.004°); crossings, T-joins (0.015°) and end-to-end joins connect roads; hubs attach to the nearest road point by splitting the edge. Added 0.16.0 (2026-10-29): a second T-join pass for still-loose ends (0.03°), a node-merge pass (the rounding cell gave two nodes at one spot on cell boundaries: 551 merged; I-94 met US-41 at the Wisconsin line twice and never joined), a graph-level pass that ties any dead end to a road within 2 mi (65 tied; dead ends 262 → 118, none within 2 mi of another road), and `CURATED_ROADS` for roads Natural Earth leaves out (US-20/26 Carey–Arco–Blackfoot, Idaho). Remaining dead ends are real ends or gaps > 2 mi.

Places (0.14.0 → 0.16.0): `packages/data/src/places.ts` generated by `packages/data/tools/build-places.mjs` from GeoNames cities500 (CC BY 4.0, scratchpad `geonames-data/cities500.txt`; Natural Earth's populated places only had 679 US towns, which is why the map looked empty). Run with `npx tsx` (it imports the road network to compute markets): 4,786 places = every town ≥ 10k (fold 5 mi, folded people counted; a county seat ≥ 0.75× keeps its name), every county seat/capital ≥ 500, every hub, half-degree coverage anchors ≥ 1,000. NYC's five boroughs are excluded by geonameid (New York City already counts them). Each row carries the road point the town joins (`atLat/atLon`): the builder scores the nearest spot on every road within 40 mi by hours to the four nearest hubs (hub Dijkstras over the unsplit graph) and takes the nearest road that isn't a detour (≤ best × 1.25 + 10 min). At runtime `RoadNetwork.addTerminal(lat, lon, at)` projects onto the road's segments and splits the edge there (`splitEdge`, geometry arrays grow; old points marked dead via `pointEdge = -1`), so routes run through towns; `buildNetwork` attaches all places before routing hub pairs. Production weight is linear in population (floor 1,000) for manufactured goods; land-based goods are per place (state table). Probe: `npx tsx apps/sim/src/traffic.ts 60` (load ends by town size and state, detours, town-to-road distance: median 0.8 mi, p90 12 mi).

Routing: cost = miles/58 (interstate) or /42 (US hwy) + 0.05 h per highway stretch + toll/120 FD per hour. Tolls per mile are 5-axle transponder rates by state/route in the build tool's `TOLL` table (PA 0.75, IN 0.55, NJ 0.55, FL 0.45, IL 0.35, OH/NY 0.3, KS 0.2, DE 1.1, Chicago Skyway 3.0, E-470 1.0). Natural Earth's Tollway tag covers the turnpike systems; the `CURATED` toll list only fills real gaps (MD JFK Highway and tunnels, DE Turnpike, NH I-95, the Skyway price, the PA I-70 overlap, WV I-77 south of Charleston). Engine: tolls charged with the miles (`sink:tolls`), in break-even and in shippers' rates; speed = route miles/hours; crash risk ×1.6 on highway share and winter gain by latitude; weigh stations matched to edges at build time and re-split with the edge.

**Why:** the road graph is the base for enforcement, tolls, the map and where trucks are seen; Jeff wants traffic in the blank parts of the map, which needs towns on the road and a connected graph. Data downloads (80 MB shapefile, 42 MB GeoNames) are not in the repo.
**How to apply:** if hubs or weigh stations change, rebuild roads.ts, then places.ts (its attach points reference the geometry). Keep the determinism rules (integer geometry, IEEE adds/divides, ties by index). A route bug found 0.16.0: `route(a, b)` returned null when a place's node was the hub's own node; now a two-stub route. Related: [[lanes-next-features]], [[lanes-phase0-state]].
