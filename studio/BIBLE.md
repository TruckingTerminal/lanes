# LANES design bible (draft 0, 2026-11-01; the director fills it with Jeff through the producer)

## The pitch, in one breath
*To write with Jeff.* Working line: you run a trucking company in one shared America on the real clock, against
everyone else; the freight market is the game, the token is the scoreboard.

## Who it's for
Dispatchers and brokers who play the load board for a living; owner-operators; the trucking-sim audience; the
crypto audience last, as a reward for a game people already play.

## Pillars (three, no more)
1. **The market is real.** Rates, hubs, docks, hours and weather behave the way they do out there; a dispatcher
   should feel at home in five minutes and find something true in every system.
2. **One world, permanent.** The clock never stops, every order stands, everyone sees the same thing.
3. **Growth is earned and risky.** Each truck, driver and business is a decision with a downside.

## The core loop (what a session of play is)
Bid on loads, watch the truck roll, get paid, reload, keep it running, grow. *The director states the
minute-to-minute, hour-to-hour and week-to-week loops here.*

## The first ten minutes
*UX and the director specify it; the slop audit feeds it.* The target: a stranger on a phone wins a load inside
five minutes, understands why it paid what it paid, and knows what to do next.

## What the game is not
Not a dashboard. Not a wallet with a game attached. Not a crime sim with trucks in it. Not a wiki.

## The cut list
*What we have that doesn't serve the pillars, and leaves or waits for a later layer.* The director proposes;
Jeff decides.

## Open questions for Jeff
- The one-breath pitch, in his words.
- Which of today's systems he'd keep if he could keep ten.
- The feeling at the end of a good session.
